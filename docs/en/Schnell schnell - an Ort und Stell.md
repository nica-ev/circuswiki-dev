# Quick, Quick – Back in Place

A reaction game for medium-sized groups.

The game leader stands in the center of a square. The players are distributed in equally sized groups along the sides of the square.

The game leader turns (90, 180, 270, 360 degrees) – now everyone must realign themselves / switch places so that the original situation is restored.
When a group is completely back in place, they call out, "Quick, quick – back in place!"
