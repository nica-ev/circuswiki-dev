> [!info] Brief Description
> A reaction game where groups quickly return to their original positions after the game leader spins.

**Group Size**: 8-30 people
**Difficulty**: medium
**Materials**: none
**Game Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A reaction game for medium-sized groups.

The game leader stands in the center of a square. The players are distributed in equally sized groups along the sides of the square.

The game leader spins (90, 180, 270, 360 degrees) – now everyone must realign themselves or switch places to restore the original situation.
When a group is completely back in place, they call out, "Quick, quick – back in line!"
