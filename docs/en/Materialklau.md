# Material Heist

A game for medium to large groups:

Game variant for 16 people:
Four hoops are distributed around the room (as far apart as possible), and one hoop is placed in the center. Four different materials are placed in the center hoop, with four of each item (e.g., 4 balls, 4 scarves, 4 sticks, and 4 rings).
The group is divided into 4 teams of 4 people each, who position themselves at the empty hoops.
The game leader announces a task, for example: "4 identical materials," "4 different materials," or "an empty hoop."
The teams must then try to retrieve the required materials from the center hoop. However, only one player from each team can go at a time, alternating turns.
The goal of the game is to be the first team to complete the task set by the game leader. Teams can hinder each other by stealing materials from other teams' hoops or by placing materials into them.
