> [!info] Brief Description
> A creative icebreaker game for small groups where participants symbolically represent their personal journey or other themes using objects, offering deep insight into their experiences.

**Group Size**: Small
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: A large box filled with toys, small animals, building blocks, Lego, and similar items.
**Game Duration**: Approx. 30-45 minutes

## **Game Description**:

An icebreaker game for small groups.

A large box filled with toys, small animals, building blocks, Lego, and similar items is placed in the center of the circle.

Each participant is given 5-10 minutes to lay out their personal journey (themes can vary).

The goal is to construct a line/sequence of objects that represents one's personal journey on a specific theme, from the past to the future, in a kind of timeline.

Afterward, each person presents their timeline, and questions can be asked.
