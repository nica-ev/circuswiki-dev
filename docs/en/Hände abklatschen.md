# High-Five Slap

**Group Size**: 5+ players
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 6 minutes

## **Game Description**:
All players sit in a circle. Each person holds their right hand out to the right, so it faces their right neighbor. Then, each player places their left hand in front of the right hand of their left neighbor. The game leader chooses a number between 2 and 21 and says it aloud. Then, they say the number "one" aloud, while gently slapping the left hand of their right neighbor (which is lying in front of their own right hand) with their own left hand. The person whose hand was just touched, i.e., the right neighbor, does the same, but they no longer say a number aloud. Everyone must now quietly count to themselves as the slaps go around the circle. When the number determined by the game leader at the beginning is reached, the person whose turn it is to slap, and their right neighbor, must act quickly. The person whose turn it is tries to slap their neighbor's hand as usual, but the neighbor tries to pull their hand away beforehand. However, they may only pull their hand away once the hand attempting the slap is no longer touching it, meaning it has "started its move." The person of the two who does not achieve their goal is out for this round. The person who remains in the game chooses a new number. Anyone who makes a mistake, such as slapping at the wrong time or pulling their hand away too early, is also out. The last player remaining wins.
