> [!info] Short Description
> "Hello, I am" is a simple and quick icebreaker exercise for groups.

**Group Size**: 4 or more participants
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Duration**: 2-10 minutes (depending on the number of participants)

## **Game Description**:
Everyone stands in a circle. One person at a time goes into the center of the circle, greets the others with an individual movement, and introduces themselves.

*E.g.: "I am Marc" + doing a forward roll*

The person steps back into the circle. Now, everyone else imitates the movement and greets them back:

--> Everyone does a forward roll and says "Hello Marc"*
