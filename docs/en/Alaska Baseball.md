# Alaska Baseball

**Group Size**: 30 to 100 players
**Difficulty**: Simple
**Materials**: None
**Duration**: Approx. 5 minutes

**Game Description**:
The group is divided into two teams of roughly equal strength. One team receives a toilet brush or another throwable object and designates the first thrower. The other team can position themselves on the field or devise another tactic. The thrower then tosses the toilet brush to any spot on the field. Afterward, they must run around all their teammates. How the teammates arrange themselves is up to them, as long as the thrower completes as many laps as possible. The other team must quickly retrieve the toilet brush, then line up one behind the other, and pass the brush through everyone's legs. When the last player holds the toilet brush, they loudly shout "Alaska Baseball." This is the signal for the other team to stop. The number of laps the thrower completed before the signal is recorded. Now it's the other team's turn to throw, and the last person in the line who shouted "Alaska Baseball" becomes the thrower. The team that threw first must now pass the toilet brush through their legs. This way, the toilet brush is passed back and forth between the two teams.

The game ends either when a specific time has elapsed, a certain number of rounds have been played, or one team has reached a predetermined number of laps.
