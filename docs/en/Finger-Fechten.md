> [!info] Brief Description
> A short partner game where two people try to tap each other's stomachs with their extended index fingers.

**Group Size**: 2+ people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 2-5 minutes

## **Game Description**:

This game can be played with any group size.

Form pairs.
The two people shake hands. Then, they take one step back, extend one index finger, and assume a "fighting stance."
On command, they try to tap their partner's stomach with their finger.
The first person to successfully tap their partner wins the game.
