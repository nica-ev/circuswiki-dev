# Human Checkers

**Difficulty**: Easy
**Group Size**: 8 to 20 players, preferably an even number
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 4 minutes

**Game Description**:
Two teams of equal size face each other on two lines. The most important rule is: no talking during the game! One player from the team that is supposed to start jumps forward or sideways with their legs together, never backward. Now it's the other team's turn, and here too, a player jumps according to the same principle. The goal of this game is to tag all other players, meaning to jump in such a way that you can tap another player. Alternatively, a player can jump over the opposing baseline, in which case that player's team wins. If a player makes a mistake while jumping (not landing on both feet, jumping backward, falling over, etc.), they are out. If two players from the same team jump in one turn, the one who jumped second is out. If a player falls over or has to support themselves while trying to tag someone, only that player is out, even if the other person was tagged. If someone falls over out of fear of being tagged or takes a step, they are out. And finally: anyone who talks or makes noise is also out.
