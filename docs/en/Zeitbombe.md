# Time Bomb

**Group Size**: 8+ players
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Depending on the group size, a few players are designated as "chasers" and marked with a cloth, jersey, or rope. If a chaser tags a player, that player becomes a "time bomb." They stretch their arms out to the sides, count down loudly from ten to zero, and slowly move their arms over their head. When they reach zero, they clap their hands once above their head, as their arms have now come together. The bomb has exploded, and the player is out. To prevent this, an un-tagged player can lift a ticking bomb and thus defuse it. The bomb is now safe! To speed up the game even more, the time bomb could tick down for only 5 seconds, for example.
