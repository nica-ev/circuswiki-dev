# Bibbel Di Babbel Di Buh

A circle game for small to medium-sized groups.

Everyone stands in a circle, and one person goes into the center.
The person in the center (P1) then points to any person (P2) in the circle. It's important to point clearly at someone and make eye contact so it's obvious who is meant.

You can then choose from the following rules:

**"Bibbel Di Babbel Di Buh"**: P1 says this phrase, and P2 must say "Buh" before P1 finishes the sentence. If P2 is too slow, P2 must go to the center.

**"Buh"**: When P1 says "Buh," P2 must not react. If P2 says anything anyway, P2 must go to the center.

**"Toaster"**: P1 says "Toaster." The people to P2's right and left turn to face P2 and hold their hands outstretched next to P2. P2 jumps up and shouts "Bing" (P2 is the toast, the others form the toaster). Anyone who does something wrong must go to the center.

**"Broken Toaster"**: This command is similar to "Toaster." The people to P2's right and left again form the toaster, and P2 is the toast. This time, however, P2 stands still, while the other two jump up and shout "Bing."

**"Washing Machine"**: The people to P2's right and left hold their hands up and down to form a large circle in front of P2, creating the washing machine. P2 circles their head within this circle – the laundry being washed.

**"Vomiting Kangaroo"**: P2 holds both hands in front of them, forming the kangaroo pouch. The two people to P2's right and left bend over the pouch and make loud vomiting sounds.
