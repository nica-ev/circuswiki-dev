# Medusa

A circle game for small to medium-sized groups:

Everyone stands in a circle, looking at the floor.
The game leader counts "1-2-3," and then everyone lifts their heads and looks into the eyes of a person of their choice.
If two people's gazes meet by chance, they "turn to stone" each other and fall to the ground with a theatrical gesture.
The last two people still standing in the circle win.
