# The Circus is in Town

**Group Size**: 10+ players
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Duration**: 5 to 15 minutes

**Game Description**:
This is a fantastic game for creative facilitators, as "The Circus is in Town" is different every time it's played, depending on the interactive story the facilitator invents. The associated interactions are best explained within the story itself. As the game's name suggests, the facilitator tells a story about a circus that has come to town. Here's a basic guide for the story: Whenever the narrator says "the circus is in town," all listeners must enthusiastically shout "Oh yes!" During the story, the facilitator describes what there is to see at the circus (e.g., jugglers, clowns, acrobats, tightrope walkers, etc.), and whenever one of these attractions is mentioned, all listeners perform a specific movement associated with it (the juggler juggles, the clown makes a funny face, etc.). Now, the story moves to the opening of the ticket booth for the circus show. This is the signal for everyone to line up at the ticket booth. The facilitator then diligently sells tickets to the waiting guests. The last person in line gets nothing because the tickets are all sold out. This last guest then becomes a lion that must go into the cage (a designated spot). The show can now begin, and the narrator can describe all the acts, with the listeners again performing the movements on cue. In addition to the cage, there are two other designated spots or corners. One has popcorn, and the other has cotton candy. If the word "popcorn" is mentioned in the story, everyone must run to the popcorn corner and shout "pop pop pop" as if they were popcorn. The last person to arrive at the popcorn corner also becomes a lion and goes into the cage. It works similarly with the cotton candy corner: if the word "cotton candy" is mentioned, everyone runs to the cotton candy corner, spins in a circle, and says "Sugar, sugar." Whoever does this last becomes a lion. The facilitator can switch between these elements in their story at any time: commands, the ticket booth, and the cotton candy and popcorn corners. If several people have already become lions, the facilitator can move on to the next option: the animal parade for the audience. During this, the lions are initially only allowed to go up to a certain line, and the audience can get very close to marvel at the lions. However, the audience must also be shown the safety line, where they are safe from escaped lions. This is because the narrator can shout "the lions are loose!" at any time. At this command, the lions sprint out and try to catch the audience members. The audience, of course, tries to get behind the safety line as quickly as possible. Whoever gets caught also becomes a lion. If there are no more audience members left to catch because everyone is safely behind the line, the facilitator leads the lions back to the cage and starts a new animal parade. If eventually, no audience members are left, there are only happy lions, and the story comes to an end.

## Inclusive

- Popcorn (hop + call "pop pop")
- Clowns (laugh)
- Cotton candy (spin)
- Tigers (hiss, creep, arch back)
