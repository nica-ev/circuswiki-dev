> [!info] Brief Description
> "I'm Lucky" is a dynamic chasing game that combines elements of tag, teamwork, and "Rock, Paper, Scissors" to encourage movement, quick decision-making, and interaction.

**Group Size**: Any
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: Any

## **Game Description**:

Children form pairs by holding hands. These pairs are the "chasers" (there can be multiple chaser teams playing at once).

When a chaser team tags another person, the three individuals involved play a mini-game:

All three stand together in a circle and say "I'm lucky." As they say this, they move their closed fists up and down, similar to "Rock, Paper, Scissors."

On the third count, each of the three players simultaneously chooses "rock," "paper," or "scissors" and shows their choice with both hands (meaning both hands as a fist for rock, both hands flat for paper, or both hands with spread fingers for scissors).

*   If two of the three players show the same thing (e.g., rock twice), these two form the new chaser team. The third person is now free and runs away from the chasers again.
*   If all three players show something different, or if all three show the same thing, there's another round of "I'm lucky" until a new chaser group is formed.

As the game leader, you can easily control how many chaser teams are in play by adding or removing teams. It's recommended to start with a few chaser teams and gradually add more.

The game doesn't have a real end, as the number of chaser teams always remains the same.
