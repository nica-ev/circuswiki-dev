> [!info] Brief Description
> An icebreaker game played in a circle where participants introduce each other based on assumptions.

**Group Size**: 5-15 people
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: Approx. 10-15 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Inspired by [Gossip in Presence](Tratschen%20in%20Anwesenheit.md). An icebreaker game for small groups.

### Variation 1

Everyone sits in a circle. One person starts, stands up, and positions themselves behind another person, then "introduces" that person to the group.
The key element – similar to [Gossip in Presence](Tratschen%20in%20Anwesenheit.md) – is that only assumptions are made about the person.

After a while, the person who was described clarifies the assumptions or corrects any inaccuracies.

### Variation 2

Everyone sits in a circle.
One person starts, stands up, positions themselves behind another person, and introduces that person to the group.
When the introducing person finishes, they take the place of the introduced person. The introduced person then takes their turn to find a new person to introduce.
