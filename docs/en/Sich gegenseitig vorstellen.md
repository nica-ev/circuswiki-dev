# Introducing Each Other

> [!info] Short Description
> "Introducing Each Other" is a creative icebreaker game for small groups that is based on assumptions and promotes participants' imagination and communication skills.

**Group Size**: Small
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: Approx. 10-15 minutes

## Game Description:

Inspired by [Gossiping in Presence](Tratschen%20in%20Anwesenheit.md). An icebreaker game for small groups.

### Variation 1

Everyone sits in a circle. One person starts, stands up, goes behind another person, and "introduces" them to the group.
The key here – as with [Gossiping in Presence](Tratschen%20in%20Anwesenheit.md) – is that only assumptions are made about the person.

After a while, the person who was described clarifies the assumptions or corrects things.

### Variation 2

Everyone sits in a circle.
One person starts, stands up, goes behind another person, and introduces them to the group.
When the introducing person is finished, they sit in the spot of the person they introduced. The person who was introduced now takes their turn to find a new person to introduce.
