# Zah

**Group Size**: 6 or more players
**Difficulty**: Quite easy
**Materials**: None
**Duration**: Approx. 5 minutes

**Game Description**:
This is also a circle game. One person standing in the circle holds their hands together, palms facing, above their head. This person has the "energy." They pass the energy on to another person with a loud "Zah," pointing with their hands above their head. The player being pointed at receives the energy. To do this, they make the opposite movement: bringing their hands together in front of their body and moving them over their head. While doing this, the player again calls out "Zah" loudly. The two neighbors of this player now "cut" them from the side. For this, both hands are brought together and moved past the player's side with a loud "Zah." No touching! If the player who has the energy is "cut," they pass the energy on to someone else in the circle, just like the first player. Once all players understand the game, you can also play Zah with elimination. If someone is too slow, forgets to call "Zah," makes a wrong movement, or makes another mistake, they must sit down. But be careful! This creates new neighbors for the "cutting" action, because only the standing players can participate. So, one, two, three, five, or ten players can be sitting in between, and you are still always the neighbor of the next standing player. Zah is most fun when it goes really fast!

**Variation**:
For those who find the command "Zah" too monotonous, you can use different sounds. The energy is sent off: Zah. The energy is received: Zii. The energy is cut: Zoh. This makes the game significantly more challenging.
