# The Stomping Bank

**Group Size**: 4+ players
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 2 to 8 minutes

**Game Description**:
All players get into a "bank" position or lie on their stomachs, forming a circle shoulder to shoulder. The left hand is placed between the hands of the player to the left, and the right hand is placed between the hands of the player to the right. Thus, at the start, each player has two other hands between their own. The game begins: one hand starts stomping on the floor. The stomping moves from hand to hand clockwise around the circle, following the order of the hands, not the people! If someone stomps twice in quick succession, the direction of play reverses. If a player misses their turn, is significantly too slow, or stomps in the wrong place entirely, that hand is out. If both of a player's hands are out, the player is eliminated, and the circle can be made smaller. The main focus of this game is the speed of the stomping!
