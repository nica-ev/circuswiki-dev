# Scarf Juggling - Fundamentals

Juggling with scarves is particularly well-suited for learning the fundamentals of throwing and catching due to the slow flight path (caused by the high air resistance of the scarves).

Often, success can be achieved very quickly, even progressing to juggling with 3 scarves.

For beginners, it is also very helpful to use different colored scarves to get a better overview of what is happening.
