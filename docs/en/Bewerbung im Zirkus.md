# Applying to the Circus

A game for medium to large groups:

All participants sit together in a circle. One applicant is chosen and must leave the room.

Next, a circus director is selected.

The applicant is brought back into the room and has three attempts to identify the circus director.

The circus director continuously initiates movements that everyone else must copy. The director must try to perform these movements as subtly as possible so that the applicant sees the same movement from all participants simultaneously.

If the applicant guesses the director, they win the game.
If they fail, the group wins.
