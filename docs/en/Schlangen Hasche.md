> [!info] Brief Description
> A ground-based tag game where caught players become snakes themselves and tag the remaining players.

**Group Size**: 6+ players
**Difficulty**: Simple
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: 5 to 10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

One player is designated as the "catcher." This player crawls on the ground like a snake and tries to tag the other players on their legs.
When a player is caught, they also become a snake.
The last player remaining on their feet wins.
