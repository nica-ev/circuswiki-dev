> [!info] Short Description
> A quiet game where the "Laughing Bear" must make lying players laugh without touching them.

**Group Size**: 6+ players
**Difficulty**: Simple
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 4 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Before the game begins, one person must be chosen to be the Laughing Bear. All other players lie down on their backs on the floor. They should all keep their eyes open and look at the ceiling. From this point on, those lying down are not allowed to speak or make any noise. The Laughing Bear must then try to make the lying players laugh, but without touching them! If the Laughing Bear succeeds in making someone laugh, that person also becomes a Laughing Bear, and they can continue as a pair until there are no players left lying down. The only important rule is that the lying players must not tense up their faces. If you have to laugh, you have to laugh!
