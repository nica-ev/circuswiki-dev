> [!info] Brief Description
> A dynamic tag game for medium to large groups that promotes spatial awareness and reaction time.

**Group Size**: Medium to Large
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 10-15 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Everyone stands in a circle, alternating their facing direction inward and outward. A rabbit and a fox are chosen. The fox tries to catch the rabbit.

The game begins with the rabbit inside the circle and the fox outside. While the rabbit can move freely in and out of the circle, the fox can only enter the circle by tapping the back of a person facing inward and taking their spot. This person then becomes the fox.

If the fox wants to leave the circle, they must tap a person facing outward and take their spot. This person then becomes the fox.

The game ends when the rabbit is caught.
