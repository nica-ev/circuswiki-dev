# Hello Neighbor

**Group Size**: 10+ players
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Duration**: Approx. 5-10 minutes

**Game Description**:
"Hello Neighbor" is a great game for loosening up the group and getting to know each other. All players stand in a circle, except for one person who walks around the outside of the circle. This player can tap someone standing in the circle, and then that person must run in the opposite direction. When the two players meet, they must very politely say to each other at the same time: "Hello Neighbor! How are you doing? Thank you, goodbye." After saying this, they can continue running in their respective directions. Whoever reaches the empty spot in the circle first gets to stand in it. The other player continues walking around the circle and can tap a new person.
