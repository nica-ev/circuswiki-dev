> [!info] Short Description
> A running game in a circle where two people politely greet each other and race for the open spot.

**Group Size**: 10+ participants
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 5 to 10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

"Guten Tag Herr Nachbar" (Hello Neighbor) is a good game for loosening up the group and getting to know each other. All players stand in a circle, except for one who walks around the outside of the circle. This player can tap a person standing in the circle, who must then start running in the opposite direction. When the two meet, they must very politely say to each other at the same time: "Hello neighbor! How are you? Thank you, goodbye." After this, they can continue running in their respective directions. Whoever reaches the empty spot in the circle first gets to stand in it. The other player continues walking around the circle and can tap a new person.
