# Card Finder

**Group Size**: 4 to 20 players
**Difficulty**: Simple
**Materials**: At least one deck of cards
**Game Duration**: 10 to 15 minutes

**Game Description**:
All players are divided into four equal groups. Each group lines up behind a starting line and is assigned a suit: spades, diamonds, hearts, or clubs. Each group should also have a group leader. Opposite the starting point, in the middle, there is a box, a stool, or a mat. On this object are several playing cards, an equal number of each suit. The more cards there are, the longer the game will last.

After the game begins, the first player from each group runs out. They may turn over one of the cards. If the card is the suit assigned to their group, they can take it with them. If the card is one of the other three suits, they must put it back and run back without taking it. Once they reach their group, they high-five the next player in line and go to the back of the line. The next player does the same.

When a card is taken, it is given to the group leader. The number of cards of each suit that should be laid out must be known, because when this number is reached, the game leader knows that the corresponding team has won.

The game ends when each team has given all its cards to its group leader.
