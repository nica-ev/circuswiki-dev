# Kataflasch

**Group Size**: 10+ players
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 5 minutes

**Game Description**:
Kataflasch is a rhythmic circle game. All players stand in a circle and place their hands on the shoulders of the person in front of them. Then, everyone starts at the same time. Everyone says "Kataflasch" together, and on "flasch," everyone steps forward with their left foot. Again, everyone says "Kataflasch" together, but this time stepping forward with their right foot. Next comes "Kataflaschflasch-flasch," accompanied by the step sequence left-right-left. Then, everyone says "Anageta-tung-tungi-tungi" together, moving their hips with each "tungi." First to the left, then to the right, and then to the left again. This is followed by "Anageta-flauti-flauti-flauti," where everyone moves their hips forward, then back, and then forward again with each "flauti." Finally, the whole sequence starts over from the beginning with "Kataflasch" and a step forward with the left foot. After a few rounds, players no longer grab the shoulders of the person directly in front, but rather the shoulders of the person two places ahead. To do this, everyone must slide slightly into the circle, as the circle becomes tighter and thus smaller. After a few more rounds, you can again move your hands one person further forward and grab their shoulders. After playing another round or two, you can try the following: Everyone simultaneously sits down on the thighs of the person behind them, without letting go of the shoulders of the person two places in front. If everything goes well, everyone can sit relatively comfortably!

**Variation**: Try playing with different moods – all sad, all fast, all in slow motion, all hyper, etc.
