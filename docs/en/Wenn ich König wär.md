> [!info] Brief Description
> A performance game where individuals act as the King, proposing activities that the group immediately enacts.

**Group Size**: 4-30 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: Platform
**Game Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Performance Game, Theater / Circus Pedagogy

One person from the group stands on a platform and calls out, "If I were King, I would..." then they name an activity they would do, e.g., "...eat ice cream all day," or "...hop on one foot," or "...transform into a unicorn."

The group then performs this activity until another person from the group steps onto the platform and calls out again, "If I were King..."
