# If I Were King...

Performing Arts, Theater / Circus Pedagogy

One person from the group stands on a platform and calls out, "If I were king, I would..." then they name an activity they would do, e.g., "...eat ice cream all day," or "...hop on one foot," or "...turn into a unicorn."

The group then acts out this activity until another person from the group steps onto the platform and calls out again, "If I were king..."
