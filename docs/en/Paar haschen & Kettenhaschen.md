# Partner Tag & Chain Tag

**Group Size**: 15+ players
**Difficulty**: Very Simple
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Under 5 minutes

## **Game Description**:

One player is chosen to be the initial "it." All other players spread out within the designated play area. When the starting signal is given, the "it" player tries to tag others. Once tagged, the tagged player joins hands with the "it" player, and they continue to tag others as a pair. As they tag more players, they form a chain of three, then four. When the chain reaches four players, it splits in the middle, creating two pairs of two players, who then continue to tag. More and more tagging pairs emerge until eventually, everyone is tagged. This game can also be played without the splitting mechanic. In this variation, the chain of "it" players simply gets longer and longer. If the chain breaks (meaning a player accidentally lets go), it must be reconnected before anyone else can be tagged.
