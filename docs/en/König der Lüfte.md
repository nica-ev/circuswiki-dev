> [!info] Brief Description
> A trust game in which one person is carried, supported, and carefully moved by the group.

**Group Size**: 6-20 people
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A trust game for medium-sized groups.

A king is chosen. The king lies flat on the floor, and their people gather around them. Now, the king is lifted up. Once the king is safely supported by the hands of their people, the king can move carefully, for example, by sitting up, lifting their feet one after the other into the air, or even doing a roll. The people must always follow the king's movements attentively to support them.
At the end, the king is carefully set back down on the floor.
