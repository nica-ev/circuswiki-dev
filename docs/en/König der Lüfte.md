# King of the Skies

Trust game for medium-sized groups:

A king is chosen. The king lies flat on the ground, and their people gather around them. Now, the king is lifted up. If the king is secure in their people's hands, the king can move carefully, for example, by sitting up, lifting their feet one after the other into the air, or even doing a roll. The people must always follow the king's movements attentively to support them.
In the end, the king is carefully lowered back to the ground.
