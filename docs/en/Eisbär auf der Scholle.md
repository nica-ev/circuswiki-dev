# Polar Bear on the Ice Floe

> [!info] Short Description
> A cooperative and competitive movement game where players work together to push a mat (the "ice floe") across the playing area, while a "polar bear" tries to stop them.

**Group Size**: 10+ players
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: One thick mat
**Game Duration**: 5 to 10 minutes

## **Game Description**:
The game leader or one player sits on the thick mat and acts as the polar bear on its floe. The ice floe starts at a starting line, and at the other end of the playing area, some distance away, is the finish line.

The players try to push the floe from the starting line to the finish line. However, the polar bear doesn't like this at all, as it just wants to be left alone. Therefore, it tries to tag the players who are pushing. Anyone who gets tagged must run all the way back to the starting line and, optionally, even perform a small penalty (e.g., five burpees or similar). Only then can they return to the floe and try to push it further towards the finish.

If the floe reaches the finish line, the polar bear concedes defeat and leaves its ice floe. With enough players, the game can also be played by two groups against each other as an ice floe race.
