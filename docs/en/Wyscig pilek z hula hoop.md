# Hula Hoop Ball Race

A good warm-up exercise that incorporates cooperation and group coordination.

## Preparation

Arrange hula hoops in two rows. Divide the group into two teams. Each team sits along its respective row.

Place a set number of balls, beanbags, or other small equipment in the first hoop.

## The Task

The group's task is to transport the items to the last hula hoop.

Rules:

*   Everyone in the group must touch every item.
*   Each item must "rest" in every hoop.
*   Participants remain in one spot for the entire duration of the game.

## Variations

You can change positions and methods for passing the balls:

*   Seated
*   Squatting
*   In a bench position
*   In a spider position
*   In a plank position
*   Backwards
