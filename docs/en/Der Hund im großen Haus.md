> [!info] Brief Description
> A narrative warm-up game where the group reenacts a short story with corresponding movements.

**Group Size**: 5-30 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: Approx. 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Everyone stands in a circle and imitates the facilitator's movements.

| Text                                  | Movement                                                                  |
| :------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| In a big, big house                   | Stretch up as tall as possible (spread arms wide)                         |
| Lived a tiny, tiny mouse              | Crouch down as small as possible on the floor                             |
| And in this big, big house            | Stretch up as tall as possible (spread arms wide)                         |
| Also lived a dog                      | Place hands and feet on the floor, lift hips into the air (dog pose)      |
| Very healthy                          | Lift the left leg high (maintain dog pose)                                |
| Really very healthy                   | Lift the right leg high (maintain dog pose)                               |
| And in this big, big house            | Stretch up as tall as possible (spread arms wide)                         |
| Also lived a snake                    | Lie on your stomach and wiggle (snake pose)                               |
| The dog got quite scared              | Place hands and feet on the floor, lift hips into the air (scared dog pose) |
| And ran away – to a new house         | Change places (quickly find a new spot in the circle)                     |

At the end of the story, everyone changes places so they are back in a circle, but in a different spot.
The story is repeated, and told faster each time.
