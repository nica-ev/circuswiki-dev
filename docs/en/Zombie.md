# Zombie

**Group Size**: 6 to 18 players
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 5 to 10 minutes

**Game Description**:
Players stand in a circle, except for one player who is the "zombie" in the center. The game leader then designates a person the zombie must slowly approach. Zombies cannot run! The player being "attacked" must quickly look at someone else in the circle. The person they look at must then quickly name someone else in the circle. That named person becomes the zombie's new "victim." However, if the person being looked at is too slow and the zombie reaches their intended victim first, the victim becomes the new zombie. The old zombie then takes the freed-up spot in the circle. The new zombie now attacks the person who was responsible for their transformation. That person can try to save themselves by looking for help from someone else. Players should already know each other's names reasonably well for this game.
