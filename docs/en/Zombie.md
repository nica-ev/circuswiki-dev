> [!info] Short Description
> A circle-based name-reaction game where a slow zombie is redirected by quick glances and name calls.

**Group Size**: 6 to 18 players
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 5 to 10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

The players stand in a circle, with one person in the middle acting as the zombie. The game leader then designates a player for the zombie to slowly move towards. Zombies cannot run! The player being "attacked" must quickly look at someone else in the circle. The person who is looked at must then quickly call out the name of someone else in the circle. That person becomes the zombie's new "victim." However, if the person who is looked at is too slow and the zombie reaches their "victim" before they can react, the victim becomes the new zombie and the former zombie takes their place in the circle. The new zombie then "attacks" the person who was responsible for their transformation. That person can try to save themselves by looking for help from someone else. Players should already be relatively familiar with each other's names for this game.
