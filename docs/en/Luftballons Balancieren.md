> A simple balancing game where balloons are balanced on different body parts.

**Group Size**: 2+ people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: Balloons
**Playtime**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A game for small to large groups.

Props: Balloons

A simple game to practice basic balancing skills.

Each person takes a balloon and tries to balance it on different body parts for as long as possible.
For example, on their nose, head, outstretched fingers, fist, etc.
