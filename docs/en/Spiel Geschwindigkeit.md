# Game Introduction: "Speed"

\[Game Overview\]

This game can accommodate a large number of participants (though a small group is also possible).

The number of rounds can be adjusted based on the number of participants and the desired game duration.

For a shorter game, aim for 10 rounds; for a longer game, play up to 30 rounds.

This is a game with a high element of luck, as it uses a standard spinning top (beigoma) and allows up to 4 people to play simultaneously.

Therefore, everyone, from beginners to experienced players, can enjoy it equally.

It's called "Speed" because it's a fast-paced game. Depending on the region, the game's name might be "XX Rounds," and the number of battles can also vary.

**【Rules】**

- 4 people play at a time.
- There is a "power match" element.
- Use a standard spinning top (beigoma).

**\[Scoring\]**

- Win: 3 points
- Draw: 1 point
- Loss: 0 points

\* Point distribution may vary by region.

**\[Gameplay Flow\]**

1. Each participant receives a card and writes their name on it.
2. Four people gather in a designated area and play one round.
3. The winner of the round takes the cards from the participants and records the score for everyone on their card (if there's a referee, they handle the scoring).
4. Participants move to a different area, and when another group of 4 has formed, they play again (it's a violation to play on the same spot consecutively).
5. Play as many rounds as indicated on the card.
6. The game concludes when a participant's card is filled with scores up to the final round.
7. The designated organizer tallies the results and announces the high scorer.
