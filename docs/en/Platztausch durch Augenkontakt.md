# Musical Chairs with Eye Contact

A circle game for small to medium-sized groups.
Everyone stands in a circle. Participants try to make eye contact with another person and confirm this connection with a slight nod of the head. Once eye contact is established, participants swap places by simply walking through the center of the circle, exchanging spots with the person they made eye contact with.

Everyone plays simultaneously, which trains attention and communication. Participants must be alert to notice who is looking at them and wants to swap places.

As an extension, the required contact can be progressively reduced. Initially, a nod is still necessary, but this refines over time until only eye contact determines whether places are swapped or not.

# Variation

The variation involves reducing the required signals for successful contact.
Ultimately, a simple glance without any further physical signals is sufficient to establish and confirm contact.
