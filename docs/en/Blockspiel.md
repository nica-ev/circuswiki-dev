# Game Introduction: "Block Game"

【Features】

*   You can play 10 to 30 games per person within the set time.
*   You can participate in the game at your own pace.
*   You can even join mid-game (a minimum of 10 games is required).

\[Game Flow\]

1.  Participants receive a card and write their name on it.
2.  Arrange yourselves on the floor in the specified order.
    ![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/39842b_c35cac3d625145f1b8446df105c2a658~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_624,h_726,al_c,q_90/39842b_c35cac3d625145f1b8446df105c2a658~mv2.webp)
3.  The first opponents place their cards on the table on the floor side and play one-on-one for one round.
4.  Whether you win or lose, record the result on your card.
5.  Line up in different rows and repeat the game until the time limit.
6.  When all games are finished, hand your card to the person in charge.
7.  After the time limit, the person in charge will tally the scores and announce the top performers.

\[How to Read the Card\]

*   It is divided into three blocks: A, B, and C.
*   The number written is the number of games played.
*   Record one of the match results (◎, ◯, △, X) in the corresponding game number.
*   Compete for the total score in each block.
    ![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/39842b_eb8b00f3b30f45d3a4589f66327d0aed~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_616,h_740,al_c,q_90,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01/39842b_eb8b00f3b30f45d3a4589f66327d0aed~mv2.webp)

\[Scoring Table\]

*   Win (Riki / Play): ◯ (2 points)
*   Draw (Tie): △ (1 point)
*   Knock opponent's piece off the playing area: △ (1 point)
*   Lose: X (0 points)
*   Win against a "Beigoma Master": ◎ (3 points) \* If two "Beigoma Masters" win against each other, it's ◯ (2 points).
    ![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/39842b_d1ed6f9abb794fa68cd6ab75e2cc1e36~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_925,h_547,al_c,q_90,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01/39842b_d1ed6f9abb794fa68cd6ab75e2cc1e36~mv2.webp)

\[Estimated Time Limit\]

With approximately 20 participants and 3 available playing areas, 1 hour is sufficient.

You can play up to 30 rounds, even with a break in between.
