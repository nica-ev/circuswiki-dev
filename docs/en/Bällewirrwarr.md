# Ball Chaos

**Group Size**: 5 to 15 players
**Difficulty**: Easy to hard (depending on the number of balls in play)
**Materials**: Balls (several of each color)
**Duration**: Approx. 10 minutes

**Game Description**:
In this game, all players stand in a circle. If the players don't know each other's names at all, the following introductory round is recommended: The facilitator starts by saying their name aloud, then passes a ball to someone in the circle. It's best to make clear eye contact before throwing and to throw the ball in an arc from below. The facilitator then sits down or places their hand on their head. The goal is simply for everyone to recognize that they have already had their turn. The person who now has the ball continues exactly as the facilitator did, going around the circle. The last person throws the ball back to the facilitator. Now that the order is clear, the sitting down/hand on head part can be omitted, and the order can be practiced. Don't forget to say the names of the other players! Once this is reasonably secure, a new order can be established. This is done using the same principle as above, but now you call out the name of the person who is supposed to receive the ball, instead of saying your own name. Alternatively, the old order can be maintained, and only the naming convention can be changed. Once the order is well-practiced, the facilitator can introduce multiple balls of the same color into the circle one after another.

**Extension**:
This is where the game really gets going. The facilitator can introduce another color into play, and the ball, or later the balls, of this color are thrown backward in the established order. Another ball of a different color can simply be thrown clockwise, and yet another ball of a different color can be thrown counter-clockwise. Now there are four different colors, and as many balls of each as the group can handle. To take it to the next level, the circle is dissolved, and all players move freely around the room! Now you have to know which ball goes to whom!
