## About the Projects

European projects, particularly those supported by the Erasmus+ program, have provided us with incredible opportunities for sharing, learning, and mutual growth. This ongoing support has enabled us to explore new methodologies, foster international collaborations, and bring innovative ideas to life.
The circus sector is a field of constant evolution. Through in-person meetings, regular online discussions, and deep collaborative work, we have all developed—not just as professionals, but as individuals. These shared experiences leave a lasting mark on our daily practices, our values, and our vision for the future of circus.
This current project, "Creative Inclusion of Special Need Groups - Much More Than Circus," lasted for one year and brought together five core organizations from across Europe. Together, we organized:
- Three international in-person events: a convention, a conference, and a training workshop.
- A series of online symposiums.
- And this Book of Case Studies.

We also invited guest organizations to contribute, offering a broader and more diverse perspective on inclusive circus practices. Our aim is to contribute to a larger movement: the ongoing work of the Functional Juggling Network and the development of a truly inclusive international circus community.
We hope the foundations laid here will lead to many fruitful collaborations in the future.
