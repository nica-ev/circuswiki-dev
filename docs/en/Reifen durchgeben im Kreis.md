All children stand in a circle, holding hands.
One or more hula hoops are placed around the circle. To do this, a child reaches through a hoop and then grabs their neighbor's hand again, so that the hoop is "hanging in the circle."

The hoop is then passed from child to child around the circle without breaking the hand connection. Each child must also step through the hoop.

**Variation**
Two or more hoops can also be used to create a "chase." The goal is then for one hoop to catch up to or overtake another.
