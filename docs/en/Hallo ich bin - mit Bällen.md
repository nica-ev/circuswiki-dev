> [!info] Brief Description
> An icebreaker exercise in a circle where names are linked to individual ball movements.

**Group Size**: 3-30 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: One ball per participant (all the same size)
**Playtime**: Approx. 10-15 minutes

## **Game Description**:

All participants stand in a circle. Each person has a ball (small or large, but all balls should be the same size).

Taking turns, each person steps into the center of the circle, performs a movement or trick with their ball, and greets the other participants with their name:

"Hello, I'm Marc" -> (Example: Throw the ball under the leg and catch it with the other hand).

Afterward, that person steps back into the circle.

Now, all other participants take one step forward, imitate the ball movement, and greet the person by name:

"Hello Marc" - (Repeat the trick).
