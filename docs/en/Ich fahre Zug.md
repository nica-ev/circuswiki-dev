# I'm Riding the Train

**Group Size**: 10 to 30 players
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: One sturdy chair without armrests for each player
**Playing Time**: Flexible

**Game Description**:
Players sit in a circle of chairs. Any player can start by standing up and moving to the center of the circle, leaving one seat empty. The game then begins: The player sitting to the left of the empty seat quickly slides to the right into that seat and says, "I'm riding the train." The next player, now sitting to the left of the newly vacated seat, slides into it and says, "I'm coming along." The third player also slides over and says, "I'm riding the black train." The fourth player slides over as well and calls out the name of a player sitting in the circle, who then takes the empty seat. The whole process then restarts with the newly freed-up chair! The player standing in the middle tries to grab a seat and sit down while the others are sliding around the circle. It's important that once the fourth player has called out a name, the seat is considered taken, even if the called player hasn't sat down yet.
