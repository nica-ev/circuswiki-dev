# **Pillow Race**

**Group Size**: 10+ players, preferably an even number
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 3 minutes

## **Game Description**:

To prepare for the game, all participants are divided into two equal groups. The teams stand or sit alternately in a circle. Two players sitting opposite each other each receive a pillow. The game leader dictates a common direction in which the pillow moves. Each pillow is passed only within its own team. Accordingly, the pillow must always be passed to the person two seats away within your own team, via your immediate neighbor. The goal of the game is to catch up to the opposing team's pillow. However, one team may not hinder the other, and the pillows may only be passed, not thrown.

## **Variations**:

The game leader can introduce the signal "Change Direction." When this signal is given, the pillow must change its direction of travel. Additionally, the command "Spin" can be introduced, where the player currently holding the pillow must pass it once around their own body.

Instead of pillows, other easily passable objects can be used.
