# **Team Rock-Paper-Scissors**

**Group Size**: 14+ players
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

All players are divided into two equally sized teams. The game leader has previously decided on three different characters, each with a specific sound and pose. For example, these could be: Dragon, Princess, and Knight. It's important that there's a logical hierarchy for who beats whom, similar to Rock-Paper-Scissors. For instance: The Dragon easily kidnaps the Princess, but the Knight slays the Dragon, and the Princess seduces the Knight. The two teams now confer to decide which of the three characters they will choose. Afterward, the teams meet in the middle of the playing area and line up facing each other, about two arm's lengths apart. The game leader counts down from three, and on zero, both teams reveal their chosen character. The team whose character beats the other team's character can now try to tag the players of the losing team. The losing team tries to escape behind their starting line on the playing field. Anyone tagged before reaching their line switches teams.

The characters can be freely chosen based on preference, mood, or a potential theme. The main thing is that the pose and sound of each character are clearly distinct from the others.
