# Rock Paper Scissors

An icebreaker game for small to very large groups.

The basis of the game is Rock Paper Scissors – there are only these three states. Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock.

Everyone walks around the room. When two people meet, they play Rock Paper Scissors. The loser attaches themselves to the winner's shoulders and begins chanting their name. This small "snake" continues to move through the room until it meets someone else and plays again.

At the end of the game, only one single snake remains, with the winner at the front, and everyone else chanting their name.
