> [!info] Brief Description
> An icebreaker game where losers become fans and a large name-snake is formed at the end.

**Group Size**: 4+ people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

An icebreaker game for small to very large groups.

The basis of the game is Rock, Paper, Scissors – there are only these three options. Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, paper beats rock.

Everyone walks around the room. When two people meet, they play Rock, Paper, Scissors. The loser attaches themselves to the winner's shoulders and begins chanting their name. This small "snake" continues moving through the room until it meets someone else and plays again.

At the end of the game, only one single snake remains, with the winner at the front, and everyone else calling out their name.
