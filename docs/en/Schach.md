# Chess

Suitable for small to medium group sizes

Two equally sized teams are formed.
A rectangular playing field is designated, with the two teams lining up on opposite edges.

The following rules apply:
* Players take turns.
* Only one person from a team may move at a time.
* A move consists of jumping off with both feet and landing safely on both feet. A lunge or any other unstable movement/landing means you leave the playing field.
* Who jumps from the team can only be decided through eye contact; no talking or gesturing is allowed.
* If multiple people jump at the same time, the game leader must decide who has to leave the playing field.
* Opponent's pieces can be captured by touching them with your hand after landing safely. These pieces must then leave the playing field.
* The team that first reaches the opponent's baseline or captures all opponent's pieces wins.
