# Rock Paper Scissors Fan Club

**Group Size**: 20+ players
**Difficulty**: Very Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 8 minutes

**Game Description**:
All players move around the room and find an opponent to play Rock Paper Scissors with. Only using rock, paper, and scissors! The loser becomes the winner's fan. The loser grabs the winner by the shoulders, follows them, and calls out their name the entire time. If someone who already has fans plays and loses, that player and all their fans become fans of the player who won. They all cheer them on in a long conga line. This continues until only one winner remains, whom everyone cheers for.
