> [!info] Brief Description
> A clapping game for two people with rhythmic text and simple movements.

**Group Size**: 2 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 2-5 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A clapping game for 2 people.

### Text

"Oh, balloon, caramel candy.
All monkeys and giraffes go
Ding Dang Dong
All pigs without legs,
All goats without wings
go Oink"

### Movements

"Oh *(Hands cupped around mouth as if shouting)*
balloon, caramel candy, all monkeys and giraffes go
*(Hands are clapped together alternately in rhythm, or with the partner's hands)*
Ding *(Hand touches head)*
Dang *(Hand touches hip)*
Dong *(Hand touches foot)*
All pigs without legs, all goats without wings
*(Hands are clapped together alternately in rhythm, or with the partner's hands)*
go Oink *(Hands next to ears, palms open to partner, thumbs touching temples)*
