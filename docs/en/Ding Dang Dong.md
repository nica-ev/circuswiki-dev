# Ding Dang Dong

A clapping game for 2 people.

Text:
"Oh, balloon, caramel candy.
All monkeys and giraffes go
Ding Dang Dong
All pigs without legs
All goats without wings
go Baa"

Below is the text with the movements:

"Oh *(Hands cupped in front of mouth like calling out)*
Balloon, caramel candy, all monkeys and giraffes go
*(Hands are clapped together alternately in rhythm, either by oneself or with a partner's hands)*
Ding *(Hand touches the head)*
Dang *(Hand touches the hip)*
Dong *(Hand touches the foot)*
All pigs without legs, all goats without wings
*(Hands are clapped together alternately in rhythm, either by oneself or with a partner's hands)*
go Baa *(Hands next to ears, palms open towards partner, thumbs touching temples)*
