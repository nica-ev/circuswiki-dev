# Big vs. Little

**Group Size**: 3 to 10 players
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 1 to 5 minutes

**Game Description**:
The title is pretty self-explanatory. The workshop leader/game facilitator lies down on a mat, and all or a number of participants designated by the "Big" person (the "Little" ones) get to lie on top of them. They can hold on to the facilitator's arms and legs or secure them in some other way. Only the facilitator's head must not be touched. The game begins, and the "Big" person must try to get all the "Little" ones off the mat. The "Little" ones must try to hold onto the "Big" person until they give up. Important: If the "Big" person gives up, everyone must let go immediately! When "Big" and "Little" wrestle, no kicking or hitting is allowed. Care should also be taken to ensure no one has anything sharp or hard in their pockets. If the "Big" person succeeds in getting all the "Little" ones off the mat, they win the round. If the "Big" person has to give up, the "Little" ones win.
