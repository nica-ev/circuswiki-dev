> [!info] Brief Description
> A physical wrestling game where one large person tries to dislodge several small people from a mat.

**Group Size**: 3 to 10 participants
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: Mat
**Duration**: 1 to 5 minutes

## **Game Description**:

The title is quite self-explanatory. The workshop leader/game facilitator lies down on a mat, and all or a number of participants designated by the "large" person are allowed to lie on top of them, holding on with their arms and legs or securing them in some other way. Only the workshop leader's head must not be touched. The game begins, and the "large" person must then try to get all the "small" people off the mat. The "small" people must try to hold the "large" person down until they give up. Important: If the "large" person gives up, everyone must let go immediately! When the "large" and "small" participants wrestle, no kicking or punching is allowed. Care should also be taken to ensure no one has anything sharp or hard in their pockets. If the "large" person succeeds in getting all the "small" people off the mat, they win the round. If they have to give up, the "small" people win.
