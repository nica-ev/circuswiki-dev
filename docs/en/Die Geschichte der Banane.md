# The Story of the Banana

Call and Response Game for Small to Large Groups

Text:
"Plant banana, plant, plant banana"
"Grow banana, grow, grow banana"
Pick banana, pick pick banana
Peel banana, peel peel banana
Slice banana, slice slice banana
Mash banana, mash mash banana
Shake banana, shake shake banana
Drink banana, drink drink banana
Surf banana, surf surf banana
Rock banana, rock rock banana
Mold the banana, mold the banana

Each sentence is played twice, the first time "normally" and the second time "crazy"
