# All Against One

A cooperative game with strong strategic elements, best played with medium-sized groups. It's not a purely cooperative game, as the name suggests – the whole group plays against a single individual.

Each person takes a hoop and everyone spreads out evenly across the playing area, leaving their hoop in place so that all hoops are distributed uniformly.

One person, ideally the facilitator at the start, steps out of their hoop and moves as far away from it as possible, leaving their hoop empty.

The Rules:

*   The single person may only walk SLOWLY. This point is extremely important; the slower they walk, the better the group's chance of keeping the game going.
*   The people in the group may move quickly or run.
*   Once you have stepped out of your hoop, you MUST enter another hoop. You are not allowed to go back.
*   Quickly jumping between two nearby hoops or occupying two hoops simultaneously is not permitted.
*   The people in the group can leave their hoops and occupy other, empty hoops.
*   A hoop can only accommodate one person.

The goal of the single person is to get into an empty hoop.

The goal of the group is to prevent this.

You'll quickly realize how difficult it is for the group to achieve their objective for an extended period. A shared strategy, and especially non-verbal communication and looking out for each other, are particularly important here.
