# What Are You Doing There?

A theater game for small to medium-sized groups.

There are always 2 people on stage, the rest watch.

Person A begins to pantomime any activity (e.g., eating ice cream, reading a book, brushing teeth, playing soccer, etc.).

Person B watches for a while, then approaches and asks, "What are you actually doing? / What are you doing there?"

Person A answers with a completely different activity.

Example: Person A is reading a book. B asks, "What are you doing there?" A replies, "Well, I'm washing my car."

Person B: "Ah, right – okay" and begins to pantomime that activity.

Now A watches for a while and then asks, "What are you actually doing?" and B again answers with a different activity, "I'm doing my morning exercises."

Rules:

Preferably use the center of the stage.

Body should face the audience.

Speak loudly and clearly.

Give the other person at least 10 seconds to act out something before you ask them what they are doing.

Allow 3-4 exchanges, then end the game. 2 new people can now come onto the stage.
