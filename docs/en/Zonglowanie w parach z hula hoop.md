# Partner Hula Hoop Juggling

A partner exercise to develop cooperation, rhythm, timing, and hoop work.

## Stage I

Two hula hoops per pair.

We practice synchronously:

1. Rolling.
2. Throwing.

## Stage II

Four hula hoops per pair.

We practice synchronously:

1. Rolling.
2. Throwing.
3. Rolling and throwing.
4. We repeat all stages asynchronously.
