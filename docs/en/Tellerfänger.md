# Plate Catcher

> [!info] Short Description
> A simple catching game where participants can avoid being caught by imitating juggling props.

**Group Size**: 6+ players
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: One plate, (optional: scarf, diabolo, or similar)
**Playtime**: 5 to 10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

One player is designated as the catcher. This player holds a plate and tries to tag the other participants. However, the others can save themselves by stretching their arm into the air, imitating the stick used for spinning a plate.

If someone is caught, they take over the plate.

Depending on the group size and age, you can introduce additional elements and catchers:

*   Scarf: Players can save themselves by placing the scarf on the ground (as if covering something).
*   Diabolo: A player can save another by holding their hand (imitating the string for the diabolo).
