**Group Size**: 6+ players
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playing Time**: Approx. 8 minutes

## **Game Description**:
The group forms a circle with one person in the center. This person wants to get out of the center, which they can do as follows: They point at someone and say, "Bille Bille Bob." The person being pointed at must try to say "Bob" before the person in the center finishes saying "Bob" from "Bille Bille Bob." If the person succeeds, the one in the center must find someone new. If they fail, they must join the circle and also try to get out of the center using "Bille Bille Bob." Below are some commands that can be introduced during the game and called out by the person in the center. For commands involving multiple people, you must go to the center if you make a mistake with the movement or sounds, or if you forget your part or perform it incorrectly.

1.  **Bob**:
    The chosen player must remain silent and still for three seconds.

2.  **Washing Machine**:
    The left and right neighbors of the addressed player form a circle with their arms, like a washing machine drum, and the addressed player turns their head within it.

3.  **Broken Washing Machine**:
    Basically the same as the Washing Machine, but the player in the center stays still while the heads and the washing machine drums of the outer players turn.

4.  **Toaster**:
    The addressed person stands stiff as a board, like a piece of toast. Their left and right neighbors form a box around them. Then the toast jumps up and loudly calls out "bing."

5.  **Broken Toaster**:
    Everything is the same as the Toaster, but the entire toaster jumps up and calls out "bing." The toast itself does not jump.

6.  **Streetlight**:
    The chosen player raises their arms and stretches out as if they were a streetlight. The players to their left and right lift their legs and pretend to pee on the streetlight like a dog.

7.  **Vomiting Kangaroo**:
    The person in the middle forms a bucket in front of them with their arms, and the two neighbors pretend to vomit into it.

8.  **James Bond**:
    The person chosen is James and holds a finger gun. The two neighbors on the right and left wave their hands in the air towards James and call out, "ohhhh James."

9.  **Blender**:
    The person in the middle holds their hands over their neighbors' heads. The neighbors spin on their own axis under each hand.

10. **Be creative and invent a new command with your group!**

## **Game Version as a Tag Game**:
Whenever the tagger tags someone, they call out one of the commands that involves multiple players. The tagged player is now caught and must perform the movement that the middle person of the command would have done. They can only be freed if other players correctly complete the command.

## Controversies
Various figures, such as James Bond, Mother with Child, etc., are viewed controversially, mostly regarding the role understanding they convey.
Personally, I think that instead of banning these figures, an alternative form like "Father with Child" or "Superwomen" would be more appropriate.
