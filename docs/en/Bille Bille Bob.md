**Group Size**: 6+ players
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playing Time**: Approx. 8 minutes

## **Game Description**:
The group forms a circle with one person in the center. The person in the center wants to get out, and they can do this by pointing at someone and saying, "Bille Bille Bob." The person being pointed at must try to say "Bob" before the person in the center finishes saying "Bob" from "Bille Bille Bob." If the targeted person succeeds, the person in the center must find someone new. If they fail, they must join the circle and also try to get out of the center using "Bille Bille Bob." Below are some commands that can be introduced during the game and called out by the person in the center. For commands involving multiple people, you must move to the center if you make a mistake with the movement or sounds, or if you forget your part entirely or perform it incorrectly.

1.  **Bob**:
    The chosen player must remain silent and still for three seconds.

2.  **Washing Machine**:
    The left and right neighbors of the addressed player form a circle with their arms, like a washing machine drum, and the addressed player turns their head within it.

3.  **Broken Washing Machine**:
    Essentially the same as the Washing Machine, but the player in the center remains still while the heads and washing machine drums of the outer players turn.

4.  **Toaster**:
    The addressed player stands stiff as a board, like toast. Their left and right neighbors form a box around them. Then, the "toast" jumps up and loudly calls out, "Bing!"

5.  **Broken Toaster**:
    Everything is the same as the Toaster, but the entire toaster jumps up and calls out, "Bing!" The toast itself does not jump.

6.  **Streetlight**:
    The chosen player raises their arms and stretches tall as if they were a streetlight. The players to their left and right lift a leg and pretend to urinate on the streetlight like a dog.

7.  **Vomiting Kangaroo**:
    The person in the center forms a bucket shape with their arms in front of them, and the two neighbors pretend to vomit into it.

8.  **James Bond**:
    The person chosen is James and holds up a finger gun. The two neighbors on the right and left wave their hands in the air towards James and call out, "Ohhhh James."

9.  **Blender**:
    The person in the center holds their hands over their neighbors' heads. The neighbors spin on their own axis under each respective hand.

10. **Be creative and invent a new command with your group!**

## **Game Version as a Tag Game**:
Whenever the tagger touches someone, they call out one of the commands that involves multiple players. The tagged player is now "caught" and must perform the movement that the center person of the command would normally do. They can only be freed if other players correctly complete the command.

## Controversies
Various figures, such as James Bond, Mother with Child, etc., are viewed controversially, mostly regarding the role understanding they convey.
Personally, I believe that instead of banning these figures, an alternative form like "Father with Child" or "Superwomen" would be more appropriate.
