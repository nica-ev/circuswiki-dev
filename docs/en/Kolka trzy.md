# Three Hoops

Also known as "Three Piglets."

## Preparation

One hoop is needed for every group of three people.

Starting position:

*   The hoop lies on the ground.
*   One person stands inside the hoop.
*   Two people stand on either side of the hoop.

## Objective

The group's objective is to be the first to reach the finish line.

Two people are responsible for lifting the hoop and placing it in front of the person in the middle. Then, the person in the middle steps into the hoop, and the entire action begins again.

## Rules

The person in the middle must not touch the hoop.
