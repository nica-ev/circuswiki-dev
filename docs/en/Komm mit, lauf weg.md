> [!info] Short Description
> A dynamic running game for larger groups that promotes quick reactions, orientation, and team spirit by encouraging participants to make rapid changes in direction and move together.

**Group Size**: 5+, suitable for large groups
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: 5 to 15 minutes

## **Game Description**:

This game is based on quick reactions and changes in direction. The basic idea is that a "chaser" tags a person or an entire team and gives a command that requires an immediate response.

**Variation 1 (Large Group Size):**

The group is divided into several teams of equal size. The teams stand in a circle. A chaser runs around the outside of the circle, past the teams. The chaser tags the last person of a team or any person in the circle and calls out either "Come with me!" or "Run away!".

*   **"Come with me!":** The tagged person (or the entire team) runs in the same direction as the chaser outside the circle and tries to get back to their original spot as quickly as possible before the chaser takes it.
*   **"Run away!":** The tagged person (or the entire team) runs in the opposite direction of the chaser outside the circle and tries to get back to their original spot as quickly as possible before the chaser takes it.

**Variation 2 (Small Group Size):**

All participants stand together in a circle. A chaser is chosen. This person runs around the outside of the circle, past the other participants. They tap one person of their choice on the shoulder and say either "Come with me!" or "Run away!". Depending on the command, the tagged person now runs either in the same or opposite direction as the chaser around the circle, and both try to reach the original spot first.
