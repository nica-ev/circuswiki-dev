> [!info] Short Description
> A simple passing game where objects are alternately passed over the head and through the legs.

**Group Size**: 5-20 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: Objects
**Game Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A simple game with objects for small to medium-sized groups.

Everyone stands in a line.
Objects are passed from front to back over the head.
The last person in line passes the objects from back to front through the legs of the person in front of them.

You can either play this as an endless game, or the objects can be set aside once they have completed a full round.
