# Frutti del Mare (Warm-up Game)

A warm-up exercise for small to medium-sized groups, especially suitable for young children.

**The Starfish**
We start lying on our backs, with arms and legs stretched away from the body in a star shape.

**The Clam**
We turn onto our sides and pull our arms and legs in, making ourselves as small as possible.

**The Seahorse**
The clam slowly awakens and stretches out straight, still lying on its side, becoming a seahorse.

**The Seal**
We plop onto our stomachs, lift our heads up, with our arms supporting us.
We wiggle our tails (or our bottoms).

The seal looks to the right, then to the left, then looks up at the sky. It reaches for the stars with one hand (the body already twists slightly) and then plops onto its back, becoming a starfish again.
The circle starts over from the beginning.
