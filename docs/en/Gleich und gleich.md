# Like Likes Like

Children move freely around the room. When instructed by a group leader, they find at least one other child who:

*   has the same hair color
*   has the same eye color
*   participates in the same sport
*   has a birthday in the same month
*   has a first name that starts with the same letter as their own
*   etc.

The instructions should be adapted to the age and composition of the group so that the corresponding individuals can actually be found. The game can be accompanied by music. As long as the music plays, the group members who have found each other walk around together and talk. Whenever the music stops, a new instruction is given, and new pairs are formed, who then walk together for a while.
