> [!info] Brief Description
> A reaction game where a group dodges slow, invisible sword movements and then reforms.

**Group Size**: 5-25 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A reaction game for small to medium-sized groups.

One player is the "laser sword bearer" – this player stands at the front facing the rest of the group. They make **slow** sword movements (striking towards the center, from right to left at head height, etc.).

The remaining players act like quicksilver – when the invisible sword approaches, the group quickly disperses at that point, only to reform into a cohesive unit just as quickly afterward.

The slow movements of the sword bearer are important so that the others have time to react.
