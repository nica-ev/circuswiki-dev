# Lightsaber

A reaction game for small to medium-sized groups.

One player is the "Lightsaber Bearer" – this player stands at the front, facing the rest of the group. They make **slow** sword movements (striking towards the center, from right to left at head height, etc.).

The remaining players act like mercury – when the invisible sword approaches, the group quickly drifts apart at that point, only to come back together just as quickly into a cohesive unit.

The slow movements of the sword bearer are important so that the others have time to react.
