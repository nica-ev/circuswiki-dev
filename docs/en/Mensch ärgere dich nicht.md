# Don't Get Angry (Mensch ärgere dich nicht)

**Group Size**: From approx. 4-6 players
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: Many rings / Hula Hoops
**Game Duration**: 20-30 minutes
**Source**: Facebook

**Game Description**:
The rings / hoops are lined up in a long (winding) line. It's helpful to connect them so they don't shift, but it's also possible without connecting them.

Two groups are formed, each lining up at one end of the ring line.

At the start, one player from each group hops off, moving from one ring to the next.
As soon as the two players meet, they play "Rock Paper Scissors" (Schnick Schnack Schnuck). The loser goes around the outside (outside the rings) back to their starting position and rejoins the end of the line.
The next player from that group can immediately start hopping.
The winner continues hopping.

The round is won when a player reaches the first ring of the opposing group.

It's good to play several rounds (3-5), although individual rounds can take quite a long time.
