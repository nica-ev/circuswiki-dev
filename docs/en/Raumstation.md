# Space Station

Space Station
Cooperative Game:

A clearly defined, small area is created: the space station.
Breathing is possible without issue within this area.
All participants (astronauts) begin inside the space station.
The astronauts want to collect space debris located outside the station (objects scattered around the space).
To do this, they must form chains (holding hands) to ensure their air supply.
However, this air supply only lasts for 10 seconds.
Anyone outside the space station for longer than 10 seconds, or not connected to the space station by a chain, will die.
Therefore, the group must decide for themselves when to break the chains.

The goal is to collect all the objects.
