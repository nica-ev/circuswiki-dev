# Pitbull

**Group Size**: 10+ players, more is better
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 7 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Two teams of equal size and roughly equal strength are formed. Two lines are marked opposite each other. Each team lines up at one of the lines. On a signal, the game begins, and the teams run towards each other. Players may only run forward, never backward. The goal is to get behind the opposing team's line while lifting as many players from the opposing team as possible. Anyone who is lifted and no longer touches the ground switches teams. If someone has been lifted, they immediately go to the line their new team is heading towards. They can only rejoin the game in the next round. Once all players have reached the other side, the next round begins.

The game ends when all players are on the same team.
