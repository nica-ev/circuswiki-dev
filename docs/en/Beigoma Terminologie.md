Various technical terms come up when playing with Beigoma. Only simple content. If you know it, it will be easier to communicate, so please use it!

## **Winding the Beigoma**

### Woman's Wind

Wind with a string, with two knots on the string about 0.5 to 1 cm apart.

This winding method is relatively common.

### Man's Wind

Wind with a string, with two knots on the string about 5 cm apart.

## **Types of Beigoma**

### Normal Beigoma

A top with a rounded tip on the Beigoma that has not been processed. Since it is a piece that has not been adjusted at all, the luck factor for victory or defeat is high. If a beginner of Beigoma also has a normal confrontation, the probability of winning is very high even if competing with an advanced Beigoma.

### Processed Beigoma

A top with the tip of the Beigoma processed. Depending on the processing method, you can make a "Riki-sesame" that spins for a long time, or you can make it a "Playing sesame" that often plays the opponent. Beigoma is used properly according to the rules of the game.

### Decoration Beigoma

A Beigoma made by decorating the surface of the Beigoma.

## **Terminology Used at the Time of Play**

["Chitchinochi"] A shout when throwing a Beigoma.

[Remove the floor] When you throw a Beigoma, the top should not fall to the floor.

[Riki Play (Riki)] The rule is that the person who spins the Beigoma on the floor for a long time wins. A Beigoma that spins for a long time is called "Rikigoma".

[Play Game (Hajiki)] The rule is that you win if you kick the Beigoma off the floor. Also, a piece that moves relatively because the central axis of rotation is turned off is called "playing sesame".

[Three-Second Rule] At the time of "Riki Play", you win if you are ahead of your opponent for more than 3 seconds. If it stops within 3 seconds, try again.

[Packan] Beigoma compete against each other, and all Beigoma are blown off the floor at the same time.

[One-Touch Available] This rule is applied at the time of "Riki Play". If you throw in the Beigoma and the Beigoma never touches, you have to throw it in again.

[Okama] Beigoma lies on the floor. Depending on the rules, you lose at this point.

## **Other**

[Honko] Serious game! If you win the game, you get the opponent's Beigoma.

[Bonus Honko] Service time for Beigoma beginners to advanced players. Beginners can get Beigoma if they win, but they will not get Beigoma if they lose.

[Angle] Throw a Beigoma at an angle.
