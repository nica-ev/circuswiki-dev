One person stands with their back to the others, with a small object (e.g., a ball) behind them.

Whenever they turn around, the others must freeze. Anyone who moves has to take two steps back (or return to the start).

The goal is to steal the ball and bring it to the starting line.
As soon as the ball is gone, the "Ice Queen" can make ONE guess as to who currently has the ball.

If she guesses correctly, she wins.
