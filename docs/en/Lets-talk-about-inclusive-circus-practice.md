## Prologue
The world of circus pedagogy is undergoing a quiet yet powerful transformation. For a long time, circus in education was primarily viewed as an activity for children—playful, physical, and focused on learning specific technical skills. However, practitioners worldwide are now expanding this narrow perspective. They are demonstrating that circus can be much more: a tool for inclusion, connection, and profound personal and social change.

This publication offers a glimpse into this evolving landscape. It features case studies from educators and facilitators across Europe who are redefining what circus can be and who it can serve. Through their experiences, we see how movement, play, and presence are being utilized to create new opportunities for learning, healing, and community building.

The case studies that follow are more than just summaries of workshops; they are reflections of this cultural shift. They provide insights into how these methods are implemented in diverse settings—from kindergartens and care homes to refugee shelters, classrooms, and gymnasiums. They consistently remind us of the possibilities that emerge when we shift our focus from performance to presence, moving from the question "Can they juggle?" to "How can we meet them where they are?"

This book is a collection of those moments and, perhaps, an invitation to create more of them.
