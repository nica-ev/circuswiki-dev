# The Tricky Water Transport

**Group Size:** 5 to 15 players
**Difficulty:** Challenging
**Materials:** Pot, string, water, and a second container
**Playtime:** 5 to 20 minutes

**Game Description:**
To prepare for the game, tie as many strings around a pot as there are participants. The easiest way to attach the strings is to first tie one string around the pot and then attach all the other strings to that one. Next, fill the pot with a measured amount of water and place a container a short distance away to pour the water into. Each player grabs a string. Players must hold the string at the very end; holding it shorter is against the rules. The goal now is to transfer the water from the pot into the container with as little spillage as possible. The pot can only be moved by the players holding the strings. Everyone must keep hold of their own string for the entire game. Tip: Not all strings need to be held taut at all times. Solution: There is actually only one way to transfer the water almost completely. To do this, the pot must be held securely by the strings attached to one side and the opposite side. At least two people, standing between those holding the pot, then swap places on opposite sides. This allows the bottom edge of the pot to be carefully pulled backward and the top edge to be carefully pulled forward as it is tilted. This enables the water to be poured out without loss, but it's still not easy.
