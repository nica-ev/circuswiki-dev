# Balloon Tag

> [!info] Short Description
> "Balloon Tag" is a dynamic tag game for medium-sized groups that combines movement and teamwork, where participants act as "balloons" and protect each other from "popping."

**Group Size**: Medium
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: Approx. 5-15 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A tag game for medium group sizes.

Depending on the group size, one or more taggers are chosen. All other participants are "balloons."

When a "balloon" is tagged, they lose their air and slowly collapse. They can be rescued by other "balloons" by being "reinflated" before they reach the ground. This is done either through a clear pumping motion or by blowing air into them. This process should take a few seconds. During this time, both people are immune and cannot be tagged.

### **Additional Rule:**
A "balloon" can only be reinflated a total of 3 times; after that, they are "broken" and remain on the ground.
To make the game more visual, participants can hold one hand behind their back as if it were a valve.

The game ends when all "balloons" have "popped."
