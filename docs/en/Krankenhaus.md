# Hospital

**Group Size**: 8+ players
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: 2-3 mats
**Playtime**: 5-10 minutes

**Game Description**:

Designate two or three mats as "hospitals" within the playing area, or mark off small zones in any way. Assign a number of "germs" to players, corresponding to the group size. The germ carriers try to infect the healthy players by tagging them. Infected players are weakened to the point where they must lie down on the ground. Infected players can be taken to a hospital by two or more healthy players, who must shout "neener-neener-neener" (or a similar sound) while carrying them. To do this, they hold the infected player by their arms and legs and carry them to the mats. Healthy players who are currently acting as ambulances (i.e., carrying someone) are safe from the germ carriers, but only as long as the ambulance is "full" (i.e., carrying a patient). If a healthy player tries to take someone to the hospital alone, they can be tagged. Germs are not allowed into the hospitals, so healthy players are safe there.
