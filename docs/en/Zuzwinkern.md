>[!info] Brief Description
>In the game "Wink," one player tries to find a partner by winking, while the others try to prevent this. The goal is to avoid standing alone in the circle for as long as possible.

**Group Size**: 9 or more players, preferably an odd number
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: Approx. 7 minutes

## **Game Description**:
All players stand in a circle, with two people standing one behind the other. One person, however, stands alone in the center of the circle. This person wants a partner too. They can wink at a player standing in the front row to signal them to come over. The person who was winked at tries to run to the person in the center. But the player behind them naturally doesn't want to be left standing alone, so they try to hold onto the person running away without having to run themselves. If they succeed, the front player stays with the person who was originally behind them. If the front player manages to reach the "winker," they stand behind them, completing the circle. The former back player is now alone and tries their luck with winking.
