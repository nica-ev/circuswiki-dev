>[!info]
>"Shark and Fish" is a simple group game where one or more sharks try to catch the fish, while the fish try to reach the other side safely. If a fish is tagged by a shark, it becomes seaweed and can then tag other fish itself. The game ends when either all fish have been caught or a set time has elapsed.

**Group Size**: 10+ participants
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Duration**: Approx. 5 minutes

# **Game Description**:

One person is chosen to be the shark. In large groups with correspondingly large playing areas (e.g., an entire gymnasium), there can be two or three sharks. The shark(s) stand on one side of the playing area, and all other participants, the little fish, stand on the other side. The shark(s) then call out "Shark!" and the fish call back "Fish!". This signals the start of the game. The fish try to reach the shark's side of the playing area. However, the shark(s) try to tag the fish. A shark may never move back towards their starting side. The fish can move back, but the shark can only move forward or sideways. If a fish makes it across the starting line where the shark(s) began, they are safe. If a little fish is tagged by a shark, they must sit down exactly where they were tagged and become seaweed. Important: Seaweed is rooted in place and cannot move, but they can tag little fish using their long seaweed fronds (arms). If seaweed tags a fish, that fish also becomes seaweed, right where the seaweed tagged them.
