# Crossed Hands

A reaction game played in a circle, suitable for small to medium-sized groups.

**Preparation:**
All participants sit in a circle with both hands placed on the floor in front of them.
Now, each person swaps the position of their hands with their neighbors on the right and left, so that their hands cross over those of their neighbors.

**Gameplay:**
Participants take turns clapping their hand on the floor once. The order of the hands on the floor determines the sequence, not the order of the participants.
If there is a double clap in quick succession, the direction of play reverses. Anyone can initiate this at any time.
If a hand is moved at the wrong time, or if a participant hesitates for too long, that hand is out of the game.

The last two participants remaining win the game.
