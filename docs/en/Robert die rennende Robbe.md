# Robert the Running Seal

**Group Size**: 4 to 12 players
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 3-10 minutes

**Game Description**:
This game is ideal for groups who don't know each other at all. One player starts by saying their name, an adjective, and an animal or profession. The adjective and the animal or profession must start with the same letter as their own name. For example: Robert the running seal. Now it's the turn of the person to the first player's right. This person repeats what the first player said and then comes up with something suitable for their own name. The third person repeats what the first and second players said and adds their own phrase. This continues until everyone has had a turn. In the end, the first player must repeat everything that was said one more time.
