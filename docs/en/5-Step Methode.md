# 5-Step Method

Functional Approach ![200](../img/5-Step.png#side_right)

## 1) **Concepts Before Components**

[[Table or Body Juggling]] activities to develop understanding and experience with the task. This can also include using the [Juggleboard](Juggleboard.md) or similar devices.

## 2) **Sequencing Ability**

[[Cow jumps over the moon]] sequence to develop the bilateral sequencing capacity required for timing the 3-ball cascade.

Classical Approach ![200](../img/Classical%203-Step.png#side_left)

## 3) **Classical Continuation**

Practicing throws with one, two, or three balls. See also classical models of learning to juggle (linear learning).
	
---

The main difference from the classical model is therefore the introduction of two new steps. The use of devices like the Juggleboard or [[Table/Body Juggling]] to understand the concept more easily, as well as Craig's clearly defined practice sequence ("[[Cow jumps over the moon]]").

> *Opinion*: I don't find it radically different; it's simply an extension of the classical model.
