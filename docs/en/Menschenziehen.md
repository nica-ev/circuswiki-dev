> [!info] Short Description
> A tactical team game where human chains pull opposing players into their own half of the playing field.

**Group Size**: 12+ players, preferably an even number
**Difficulty**: Medium, better suited for older participants
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: 5 to 9 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Two teams are formed. A line divides the playing field into two halves. Each team can move freely within its own half. To enter the opposing half, players must remain connected as a chain to at least two people from their own half. In this way, teams can try to pull opposing players into their own half, thereby winning them over to their team. However, as long as a player is still connected to their own half in the form of a human chain, they do not yet belong to the other team. The goal is to get all players onto one side.
