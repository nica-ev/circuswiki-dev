# Human Tug-of-War

**Group Size**: 12+ players, preferably an even number
**Difficulty**: Medium, better suited for older participants
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 5 to 9 minutes

**Game Description**:
Two teams are formed. A boundary line divides the playing field into two halves. Each team can move freely within its own half. To enter the opposing team's half, players must form a chain of at least two people connected to their own half. In this way, teams can try to pull players from the opposing team into their own half, thus winning them over to their own team. However, as long as a player is still connected to their own half in the form of a human chain, they do not yet belong to the other team. The goal is to get all players onto one side.
