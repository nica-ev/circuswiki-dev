> A short clapping game for two people, involving rhythmic alternation between palms, backs of hands, and fists.

**Group Size**: 2 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 2-5 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A clapping game for 2 people.

### Lyrics

Schoko Schoko La La
Schoko Schoko De De
Schoko La
Schoko De
Schoko La De

On "Schoko," clap your hands together, palms facing.
On "La," clap the backs of your hands together.
On "De," clap your fists together.
