# Knights and Dwarves

**Group Size**: 8 to 20 players
**Difficulty**: Simple
**Materials**: One stick (broom handle)
**Playtime**: 5 to 10 minutes

**Game Description**:
The group stands together in a huddle. The participants are dwarves who want to protect their treasure behind them.
The game leader (or one person from the group) is the knight who fights against the dwarves to steal their treasure.
The knight has a stick that they can swing either above (over the dwarves' heads, but with a safe distance), below (along the ground), or through the middle (also indicated with a safe distance) of the dwarf huddle. The dwarves must react to this.
If the stick is swung above, they must duck.
If the stick is swung below, they must jump.
If the swing comes through the middle, the huddle must split in the middle, but then immediately close again so the knight cannot get through to the treasure.
If a dwarf reacts incorrectly or too late, they are out.
The game is won when all dwarves are eliminated and the knight reaches the treasure.
