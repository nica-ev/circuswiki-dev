# The Circus Comes to Town

A participatory game for medium to large groups of children
Various commands are briefly explained:

1. "The circus is coming to town" --> Everyone shouts "Hooray!" loudly and throws their arms in the air.
2. "There's popcorn" --> Everyone jumps in place and shouts "Pop Pop Pop Pop."
3. "There's cotton candy" --> Everyone spins around.
4. "There are sausages" --> Everyone lies on the floor and rolls back and forth.
5. "The acrobats are here" --> Everyone does jumping jacks.
6. "The clowns are here"

This list can be expanded/modified as desired.
The "final station" is important.

1. "The lions are being fed" --> Everyone goes to the lion enclosure and feeds the lions.
2. "The lions are loose!" --> The lions break out and try to catch children. Captured children also become lions. There is a "safe zone," as far away as possible, where the children can escape.

At the beginning, one or more lions are designated (preferably adults/trainers – but a few children can also be chosen).

The game is well-suited for large groups if there is enough space.

# Variations on the Theme

The basic principle is also well-suited for thematic variations.

Instead of a circus with different stations, you could use a pirate crew, a Native American village, etc.
