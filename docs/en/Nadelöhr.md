> [!info] Brief Description
> A slow perception game played in a circle, where everyone, with eyes closed, carefully seeks the opposite side.

**Group Size**: 5-30 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A game for small to large groups.

Well-suited as preparation for the game [Potato Potato](Kartoffel%20Kartoffel.md).

Everyone stands in a circle.
Important:
Movement should be very slow. It's good to emphasize beforehand that in this game, participants move in slow motion, like snails that can only move extremely slowly.

Each person should find a spot on the opposite side of the circle. Then, close your eyes and visualize the circle. On command, everyone walks SLOWLY and tries to reach the other side.
When you think you've reached the other side, turn around and open your eyes.

The goal is then to form a circle again as a group.
Important: Keep your eyes closed the entire time until you have reached the other side.
Proceed carefully and slowly.

The game is called "Needle's Eye" because everyone meets in the middle and must try to pass each other with their eyes closed without losing their orientation.
