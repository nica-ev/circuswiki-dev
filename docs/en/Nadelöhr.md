# The Eye of the Needle

A game for small to large groups.

Well-suited as preparation for the game [Potato Potato](Kartoffel%20Kartoffel.md).

Everyone stands in a circle.
Important:
Movement should be very slow. It's a good idea to mention beforehand that in this game, participants move in slow motion, like snails, and can only move extremely slowly.

Each person should choose a spot on the opposite side of the circle. Then, everyone closes their eyes and visualizes the circle. On command, everyone walks SLOWLY across, trying to reach the other side.
When you think you've reached the other side, turn around and open your eyes.

The goal is for the group to form a circle again afterward.
Important: Keep your eyes closed the entire time until you have reached the other side.
Proceed carefully and slowly.

The game is called "The Eye of the Needle" because everyone meets in the middle and must try to pass each other with their eyes closed without losing their orientation.
