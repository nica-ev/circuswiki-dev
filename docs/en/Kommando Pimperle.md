# Command Pimperle

A game for any group size:

Everyone sits down in a circle.
Hands are stretched into the middle, and index fingers drum on the floor.
The game leader can now give various commands:
- Flat (fingers are placed flat on the floor)
- Back (fingers are placed upside down on the floor)
- Bridge (hands form a bridge on the floor)
- Stone (fists are placed on the floor)
- Pole (fingers point to the air)
However, these commands may only be executed if the game leader says the word "Command" before each announcement (e.g., "Command Stone").
If they don't say it, but only the command itself (e.g., "Stone"), drumming on the floor with the fingers must continue. The game leader may always execute the command themselves to confuse the other players.
If someone makes a mistake, they must run around the circle for one round.

Variation: The game can also be played standing up.
Instead of drumming, players run in place. The commands are then executed with the body.
- Flat (lie down on your stomach)
- Back (lie down on your back)
- Bridge (place hands and feet on the floor, arch your back upwards)
- Stone (kneel on the floor and make yourself very small)
- Pole (stretch arms upwards, legs together)
After each command is executed, players continue running in place.

The commands can also be adapted to the age group.
For very young children, for example, animal movements (frog, snake, elephant...) can be used.
