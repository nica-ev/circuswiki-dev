# Task Game

A game for any group size:

Everyone walks around the room.

The game leader calls out a task, e.g., "4 people, 3 heads, 6 legs."

Now, players must quickly find groups of 4. They must place their heads on the floor and also touch the floor with their feet. However, two people may only stand on one leg to fulfill the "6 legs" requirement.

The next task is: "2 people, 2 legs, 4 hands, 2 noses."

Now, pairs of 2 must be formed. Both people must stand on one leg, placing their hands and noses on the floor.

...

The tasks can start simple and increase in difficulty from round to round.

The game can end when a task can no longer be completed by everyone.
