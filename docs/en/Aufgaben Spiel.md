# Task Game

A game for any group size:

Everyone walks around the room.

The game leader announces a task aloud, e.g., "4 people, 3 heads, 6 legs."

Now, players must quickly form groups of four. They must place their heads on the floor and also touch the floor with their feet. However, two people may only stand on one leg to fulfill the "6 legs" requirement.

The next task is: "2 people, 2 legs, 4 hands, 2 noses."

Now, pairs of two must be formed. Both people must stand on one leg, placing their hands and noses on the floor.

...

The tasks can start simple and increase in difficulty from round to round.

The game can end when a task can no longer be completed by everyone.
