# NICA e.V. - Network for Inclusive Circus Arts

NICA e.V. positions itself as a network dedicated to the dissemination and development of inclusive circus education methods.

Our work is grounded in project-based activities, research, development, and knowledge transfer.

We foster regional, state-level, European, and international networks for inclusive circus education and implement projects with diverse target groups.

In partnership with universities, we evaluate inclusive circus education methods, integrating and further developing various approaches. Through further training, workshops, and the incorporation of these methods into learning environments, we actively promote knowledge transfer.

We operate on both regional and international levels.
