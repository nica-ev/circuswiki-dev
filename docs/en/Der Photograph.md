# The Photographer

A theater/circus pedagogy game for small to medium-sized groups.

The group moves freely around the performance space. On a signal, the task is to create a tableau representing a theme in the center of the stage (movement is frozen).
The finished image is then "photographed."

After a few rounds, the group sits down together to analyze the "photographs": the composition of the image, the distribution of people in the space, the visibility of individuals, etc.

Possible themes: Fairy tales, the circus, cleaning, everyday office life, shopping, a playground, at the beach, etc.
