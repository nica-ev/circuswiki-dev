> [!info] Brief Description
> A fast-paced tag game for small to medium-sized groups where two teams compete for the best capture time.

**Group Size**: Small to medium
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: Approx. 15-20 minutes

## **Game Description**:

In this tag game, two teams are formed: Team A and Team B. Team A enters the playing field, while Team B lines up outside the field.

After the starting signal, one person from Team B at a time may enter the field and try to tag someone from Team A. As soon as a person is tagged, the person from Team B must leave the field and high-five the next player from Team B before they are allowed to enter the field.

The tagged person leaves the playing field and sits to the side.

Time is stopped once all players from Team A have been tagged. The teams then switch positions (Team B enters the field, Team A goes outside).

The team with the best time wins.
