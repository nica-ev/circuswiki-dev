> [!info] Brief Description
> "Shadow Running" is a movement game where partners imitate each other's movements.

**Group Size**: Any
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: Variable

## **Game Description:**
Form pairs.
One partner starts and moves freely around the space (moving arms and legs, dancing, or similar).
The other person imitates the partner's movements, thus becoming their shadow.
After a certain amount of time, switch roles.
