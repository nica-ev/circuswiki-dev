# Feeling with Hands

A relaxation game for any group size:

Form groups of 4-5 people.

One person lies flat on their stomach and closes their eyes.

The others nonverbally agree on how many hands they will place on the lying person's body simultaneously.

The person on the floor must then guess how many hands are on their body.

Depending on the time available and how the participants feel, several rounds can be played. Then, switch roles.
