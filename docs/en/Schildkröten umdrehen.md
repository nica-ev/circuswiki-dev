# Flipping Turtles

**Group Size**: 6 to 30 players
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Duration**: 5 to 10 minutes

**Game Description**:
Depending on the players' strength and size, one or two "turtle flippers" will be designated. All other players lie down on their stomachs to become turtles. They can move around, but only while lying down! The turtle flipper(s) try to flip the turtles onto their backs. As soon as a turtle's back touches the ground, that turtle also becomes a turtle flipper. The turtles naturally don't want this to happen, so they try to hold onto each other or develop other tactics to avoid being flipped. The game ends when there are no more turtles left.
