# Good Sides

> [!info] Short Description
> "Good Sides" is an appreciative game for medium-sized groups that promotes self-awareness and awareness of positive qualities and abilities in others.

**Group Size**: Medium
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: Small slips of paper, pens, a box or basket
**Playtime**: Approx. 10-30 minutes, depending on group size

## **Game Description**:

The facilitator writes each person's name on a separate slip of paper. Additionally, for each name, they note down two positive qualities and two particularly strong skills of that individual. Older children, adolescents, and adults can and should assess themselves and write down their own qualities/skills.

The slips of paper are folded, collected in a box or basket, and mixed thoroughly. Then, one slip at a time is drawn and read aloud.

The group guesses together and explains their reasoning to identify the person described on the slip. If a person wrote their own slip, they can subtly join in the guessing to avoid being identified by their silence.

Through this exercise, participants learn about their positive attributes and how accurately they perceive themselves or are perceived by others.
