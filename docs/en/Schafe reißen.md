>[!info]
>"Wolf Hunt" is a game where the sheep try to reach the wolves' side safely, while the wolves try to catch the sheep and also reach the other side. If a sheep is pulled across the finish line by a wolf, it becomes a wolf itself. The game ends when all sheep have become wolves.

**Group Size**: 10+ participants
**Difficulty**: Quite easy
**Materials**: None
**Duration**: 3 to 5 minutes

# **Game Description**:

A small pack of wolves is designated, appropriate to the group size. They start on one side of the playing field, while all others are sheep and start on the opposite side. The wolves then make a dangerous wolf howl, and in response, the sheep bleat loudly – the round begins. The sheep try to reach the wolves' side; once they cross the line, they are safe. However, the wolves, who can only move forward and sideways, also try to reach the opposite side while catching the sheep. "Catching" in this game means pulling or carrying the sheep across the wolves' finish line. If a sheep is pulled across the line that the wolves must reach, it becomes a wolf itself. The game ends when all sheep have become wolves.
