# T-Shirt in the Pants

**Group Size**: 6+ players
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 5-15 minutes

**Game Description**: A group of "taggers" is chosen from the players. Depending on the number of players, there can be two, three, or four taggers. As a distinguishing mark, the taggers tuck their T-shirt or top into their pants. The tagger group is given a ball, which they must pass among themselves. Neither the ball nor a player alone can tag anyone. However, a tagger holding the ball cannot move. Only players who are not holding the ball can be tagged. The taggers must therefore move towards the free players, receive a pass, and then tag a player with the ball. Anyone who is tagged also tucks their top into their pants and joins the tagger team. The game ends when only one player remains untagged on the field.

For larger groups, multiple balls can be provided to the tagger team.
