> [!info] Brief Description
> A tag game with a ball, where the tagging team can only tag while in possession of the ball and gradually grows.

**Group Size**: 6+ participants
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 5-15 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A group of taggers is chosen from the participants. Depending on the number of players, this can be two, three, or four taggers. The taggers tuck their t-shirt or top into their pants as a distinguishing mark. The tagging group receives a ball, which they must pass to each other, as neither the ball nor a player alone can tag anyone. However, the tagger who has the ball can no longer move. Only players who are not tagged can be tagged by the tagger holding the ball. Therefore, the taggers must move towards the untagged players, receive a pass, and then tag someone with the ball. Whoever is tagged also tucks their top into their pants and joins the tagging team. The game ends when only one untagged player remains on the field.

For larger groups, multiple balls can also be provided to the tagging team.
