# Shake It Game

**Group Size**: Multiple groups (group size doesn't matter)
**Difficulty**: Very easy
**Materials**: None
**Duration**: 5 to 10 minutes

**Game Description**:
The Shake It Game is a call-and-response chant with corresponding movements following this pattern:

Group A: Hey Group B (name of the other group/workshop/etc.), do you want to play the Shake It Game with us?

Group B: Oh Yeeeees!

A + All: You're cool!

All: And shake it right, shake shake, shaaaake shake shake. (Shake everything to the right)

And shake it left, shake shake, shaaaake shake shake. (Shake everything to the left)

And shake it up, shake shake, shaaaake shake shake. (Shake everything upwards)

And shake it all, shake shake, shaaaake shake shake. (Shake everything)

Group B: Hey Group C (name of another group/workshop/etc.), do you want to play the Shake It Game with us?

Group C: Oh Yeeeees!

And so on, until every group has had a turn. The tempo can also be gradually increased as the game progresses.
