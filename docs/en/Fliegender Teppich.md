# Flying Carpet

**Group Size:** 4 to 20 players
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Materials:** A tarp large enough for the group size
**Playtime:** 3 to 10 minutes

**Game Description:**
All players stand on a tarp. The smaller the tarp, the more challenging the game becomes. The tarp is a lightning-fast flying carpet, but it's only lightning-fast if used correctly. Unfortunately, the players boarded the carpet on the wrong side, and "Oh no!": As luck would have it, they're already in the middle of the ocean. They must now flip the carpet without stepping off. The goal is to turn the tarp over so the bottom side is on top and the top side is on the bottom, without any player touching the ground with their feet or hands.
