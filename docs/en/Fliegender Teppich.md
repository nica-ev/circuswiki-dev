> [!info] Brief Description
> A cooperative task where the group must flip a tarp without touching the ground.

**Group Size**: 4-20 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: Tarp of appropriate size for the group
**Playtime**: 3-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

All players stand on a tarp. The smaller the tarp, the more difficult the task will be. The tarp is a flying carpet, but it only flies quickly if used correctly. Unfortunately, the players boarded the carpet on the wrong side. "Oh no!" As luck would have it, they are already in the middle of the ocean. Now they must flip the carpet without disembarking. The goal is to turn the tarp over, bringing the underside to the top and the top side to the bottom, without any foot or hand touching the ground.
