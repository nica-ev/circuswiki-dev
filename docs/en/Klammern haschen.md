# Catch the Clothespins

**Group Size**: 5+ players
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: Clothespins
**Playtime**: 4-6 minutes

## **Game Description**:

All participants in the game attach a certain number of clothespins to their clothing, preferably on their t-shirt or top. Now, everyone tries to snatch clothespins from the others. However, no one is allowed to be held! If you manage to grab a clothespin, you can attach it to your own collection. When the game ends, everyone counts their clothespins, and the person with the most wins.

**Alternative**:
This game can also be played as a team game. All players are divided into two or more equally sized teams. Each group receives a certain number of clothespins of the same color, but at least as many as there are players on the team. Each group will then have a different color of clothespins. After the game begins, the teams try to snatch the clothespins from the other teams and collect them in their own "camp." The first team to run out of clothespins loses. The team that still has their own clothespins at the end wins accordingly.
