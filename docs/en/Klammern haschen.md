> [!info] Short Description
> A movement game using clothespins, where everyone tries to grab and collect pins.

**Group Size**: 5+ players
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: Clothespins
**Game Duration**: 4-6 minutes

## **Game Description**:

All participants in the game attach a certain number of clothespins to their clothing, preferably on their t-shirt or top. Now, everyone tries to grab pins from the others. However, no one is allowed to be held! If you manage to snatch a pin, you can attach it to your own. When the game ends, everyone counts their pins, and the person with the most wins.

## **Variations**:

Alternatively, the game can also be played as a team game. All players are divided into two or more equally sized teams. Each group receives a certain number of pins of the same color, but at least as many as there are players on the team. Each team then has a different color of pins. After the game starts, the teams try to grab the pins from the other teams and collect them in their own "camp." Whoever runs out of pins first loses. Whoever still has their own pins at the end wins accordingly.
