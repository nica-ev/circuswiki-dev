> [!info] Brief Description
> A short inclusion game where pairs find one similarity and one difference during a conversation.

**Group Size**: 4-30 people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: Music
**Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A simple game focused on inclusion.

- Walk around the room to music.
- When the music stops, find a partner and talk.
- Two questions: Find one similarity and one difference.
- Repeat this several times.
