> [!info] Brief Description
> A tag game where players are "nailed down" by having their feet tapped and continue playing with restricted movement.

**Group Size**: 5-25 people
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: Approx. 15-20 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Everyone runs around the room, trying to tag each other's feet. If a foot is touched, it is considered "nailed down" to the floor and cannot be moved.

If both feet are nailed down, players can still try to tag other players who are running around.

The last person still able to move freely wins.
