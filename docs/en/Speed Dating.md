# Speed Dating

- 2 circles of equal size based on the number of participants
- 1 outer circle, 1 inner circle
- Stand facing your partner (everyone in the inner circle faces someone in the outer circle)
- The outer circle faces inward, the inner circle faces outward.

Now, do the following:
- Each person in the inner circle says their name to their partner in the outer circle and shares one piece of information about themselves.
- Then, the inner circle moves one person to the right (clockwise).

Once everyone has gone around once:
- The outer and inner circles switch places.

Afterward, lay out slips of paper with the participants' names and pens, and write down everything you can still remember on the respective slips.
