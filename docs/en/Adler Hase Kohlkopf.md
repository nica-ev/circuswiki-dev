# Eagle - Rabbit - Cabbage

**Group Size**: 8+ players
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 5-15 min

**Game Description**:

First, designate one person as the rabbit and another as the eagle among the children. Everyone else is a cabbage and sits on the floor. The basic principle is that the eagle tries to catch the rabbit. If the eagle succeeds, the two switch roles. The former rabbit becomes the eagle and must catch the former eagle, who is now the rabbit.

However, the rabbit can hide behind any cabbage at any time and tap it. This cabbage then becomes the new eagle, the former eagle becomes the rabbit and must flee or find a new cabbage to hide behind. The rabbit who tapped a cabbage becomes a cabbage and takes the place of the cabbage that was tapped.

As the game progresses, multiple eagle-rabbit pairs can be designated, but be careful: each eagle may only catch the rabbit assigned to them.
