# Three-Country Battle

Three relatively small, equally sized playing fields are marked out on a mat (larger areas can be used depending on the number of participants).

Everyone starts in the middle field.
Wrestling and grappling are allowed and encouraged. Pushing, shoving, and pulling (not by clothing) are welcome.

The goal is to push other participants out of the playing field or into the next one.
Those in the second or third field can also actively pull participants from the previous fields towards them.

The winner is the last person remaining in Field 1.

Important: The game leader must be very mindful to ensure everything stays within a playful context and that the grappling does not escalate into violent attacks.
