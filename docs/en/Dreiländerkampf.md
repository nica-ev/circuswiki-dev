> [!info] Brief Description
> A physical wrestling game played on three marked fields, where everyone tries to stay in the first field.

**Group Size**: 4-20 people
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: Mat area, playing field markings
**Game Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Three relatively narrow, equally sized playing fields are marked out on a mat (or larger areas depending on the number of participants).

Everyone starts in the middle field.
Wrestling is allowed and encouraged. Pushing, shoving, and pulling (not by clothing) are welcome.

The goal is to push other participants out of the playing field or into the next field.
Those in the second or third field can also actively pull participants from the previous fields towards them.

The winner is the last person remaining in Field 1.

## **Notes**:

Important: The game leader must be very mindful to ensure everything stays within a playful context and that the wrestling does not escalate into violent altercations.
