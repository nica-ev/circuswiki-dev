# Throwing the Ball in a Star Pattern

A circle game for small groups using balls.

The group stands in a circle. Initially, only one ball is in play. Depending on the number of people, you throw the ball to a partner standing X places to your right.

This person then throws to someone X places to their right, and so on.

It's important that everyone receives the ball once before it returns to the first person.

Each person therefore has exactly one person from whom they receive the ball, and one person to whom they throw the ball.

The pattern is completed a few times with one ball, and then the number of balls in play can be gradually increased.

This game trains coordination and teamwork, and it's also good preparation for throwing juggling and partner work.
