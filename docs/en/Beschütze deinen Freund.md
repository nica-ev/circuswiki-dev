# Protect Your Friend

**Group Size**: 8+ players
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 4-9 minutes

**Game Description**:
All players stand in a circle. One person is designated as the "chaser" and stands in the center of the circle. The chaser can then try to tag players standing in the circle. However, players can protect themselves by pairing up. If three players stand together, the chaser can choose one of the three to tag. If two players pair up and the chaser cannot tag them, the chaser can spin around once. During this moment, the pair must separate, and both players must quickly form a pair with a different neighbor. Otherwise, they can be tagged. If someone is tagged, they must go into the circle, and the chaser can take their position.
With larger groups, multiple players can stand in the circle and try to tag the outer players.
