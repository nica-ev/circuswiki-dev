> [!info] Brief Description
> A themed tag game where tagged individuals are transformed into circus characters and freed through specific actions.

**Group Size**: 6+ players
**Difficulty**: Simple
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 10-15 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Everyone is both a tagger and a rescuer.

All players run around the room and can tag anyone. When someone is tagged, the tagger can "enchant" them into one of four circus roles: an acrobat, a juggler, a clown, or a ringmaster.

The **acrobat** performs a physical movement, like jumping jacks. They can be freed when another player stands next to them and bows together with them.

The **juggler** mimes juggling. They are freed from their enchantment when another player stands facing them and they mime throwing "balls" back and forth to each other.

The **ringmaster** cheerfully calls out, "Welcome, everyone!" They are freed when someone crawls through their legs.

The **clown** stands sadly, pretending to cry. They can only be freed if someone makes them laugh, for example, by making funny faces.
