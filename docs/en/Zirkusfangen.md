# Circus Tag

**Group Size**: 6+ players
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 10-15 minutes

**Game Description**:

Everyone is both a tagger and a rescuer.

Everyone runs around the room and can tag anyone. When someone is tagged, the tagger can "enchant" them. They can be turned into an acrobat, a juggler, a clown, or a ringmaster.

The acrobat performs an athletic movement, such as jumping jacks. They can be freed when another person stands next to them and they both bow together.

The juggler juggles in mime. They are freed from their enchantment when another person stands facing them and they mime throwing "balls" back and forth to each other (passing).

The ringmaster cheerfully calls out to the group, "Welcome, everyone!" They are freed when someone crawls through their legs.

The clown stands sadly and cries. They can only be freed by making them laugh, for example, by making funny faces.
