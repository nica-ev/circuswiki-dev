Groups of 5 participants
who try to cross a lake (the playing field) using 6 pieces of paper and reach an island (a soft mat).

If someone falls into the water, there are several options:
- The group must start over.

**Goal**:
- All participants and all groups reach the island and stay on the island together.

**Variation**:
- Certain treasures or items can be collected on the way to the island.

**Time**: 15-20 minutes, depending on group size and room size.
