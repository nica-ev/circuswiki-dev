> [!info] Brief Description
> A team task where small groups cross a lake together using paper islands and reach an island.

**Group Size**: Groups of 5 people
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: Paper, soft mat, optional objects
**Playtime**: 15-20 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Groups of 5 participants attempt to cross a lake (the playing field) using 6 pieces of paper and reach an island (a soft mat).

If someone falls into the water, there are several options:
- The group must start over.

The goal is for all participants and all groups to reach the island and fit on it together.

## **Variations**:

Certain treasures or objects can be collected on the way to the island.
