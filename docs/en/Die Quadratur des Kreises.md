# Squaring the Circle

A cooperative game for small to medium-sized groups.

Props: One or more ropes tied together to form a large circle.

To begin the game, everyone stands in a circle, ensuring there's enough space between neighbors.
The rope should be long enough to go around this circle once.

If participants find it difficult to keep their eyes closed for extended periods, blindfolds can be used.

Each participant is then given the rope, holding it so that the rope runs along the inside of the circle.

The task is now to form a square, with the rope kept taut.
All participants must continuously hold the rope with at least one hand, but their positions can be changed at any time.
When the group decides they are finished, everyone can remove their blindfolds or open their eyes to see the result.
It is recommended to debrief the process together afterward.

Goals:
Cooperation, trust, perception, communication, conflict resolution, problem-solving strategies

Time required:
Highly variable, from 5 minutes to 1 hour, depending heavily on the group. In exceptional cases, no solution may be found, and the game must be stopped.

Number of participants: 10-15 is a good group size, but it can also be played with more or fewer. This is something to experiment with.

Notes:
Choose a quiet environment if possible.
Conflicts may arise.
Pay attention to safety, as there is a risk of tripping and injury.
