# Everyone Catches Everyone

**Group Size**: 10+ participants
**Difficulty**: Very Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 3 minutes

## **Game Description**:
This game is quick to explain and usually just as quick to play, but it gets the group moving. As the name suggests, everyone tries to catch everyone else. However, a player can only tag someone and be tagged themselves in the area between the shoulder blades. Anyone who is tagged simply sits down and waits until the round is over. The round ends when only one player is left standing. Standing with your back to a wall or lying on the floor is naturally FORBIDDEN!

**Extension 1**: Additionally, players who are sitting down can also tag others. These players may then tag participants anywhere on their body.

**Extension 2**: For those who find the normal version ends too quickly, you can introduce the rule that sitting players can free themselves by tagging a standing player. For sitting players, just like in Extension 1, the shoulder blade rule does not apply; they may tag other players anywhere.
