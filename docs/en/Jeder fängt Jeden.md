> [!info] Short Description
> A quick tag game where everyone is "it" and players can only be tagged between the shoulder blades.

**Group Size**: 10+ participants
**Difficulty**: Very Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 3 minutes

## **Game Description**:
This game is quick to explain and usually just as quick to play, but it gets the group moving. As the name suggests, everyone tries to tag everyone else. However, a player can only tag someone in the area between their shoulder blades and can only be tagged there themselves. Anyone who is tagged simply sits down and waits until the round is over. The round ends when only one player is left standing. Standing with your back against a wall or lying on the floor is naturally FORBIDDEN!

**Variation 1**: Additionally, players who are sitting down can also tag others. These players may then tag others anywhere on the body.

**Variation 2**: For those who find the normal version ends too quickly, you can introduce the rule that sitting players can free themselves by tagging a standing player. As in Variation 1, the shoulder blade rule does not apply to sitting players; they may tag other players anywhere.
