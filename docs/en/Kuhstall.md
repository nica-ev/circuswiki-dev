# Cow Shed

A game for medium to large groups:

Form groups of three. Two people form a "shed" (stretch their arms diagonally upwards to meet the other person's hands, forming a roof). A third person stands underneath; this person is the "cow."
One person is left over. They now try to take a spot in one of the three-person groups. They can use three commands to do this: "Cow," "Shed," or "Cow Shed."

When "Cow" is called, all cows must quickly run out of their sheds and find a new one. During this time, the leftover person can quickly enter a shed and take a cow's spot. The cow left without a shed can then call out a new command.

When "Shed" is called, the sheds must break apart and form a new roof with a different cow and a different person.

When "Cow Shed" is called, all groups of three must dissolve and find new spots. Roles can also be swapped during this. Cows can become sheds, and sheds can become cows.
