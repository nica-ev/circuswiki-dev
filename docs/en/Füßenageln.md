> [!info] Short Description
> A movement game where everyone tries to "nail down" other players' feet while staying mobile themselves.

**Group Size**: 5+ people
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Each player tries to tap the feet of other players with their hands. It's essentially everyone against everyone else.

If a player manages to tap another player's foot, that foot is considered "nailed down" to the ground. This means the player can no longer move that foot. However, they can still try to dodge with their other, free foot. Players can also tap other feet at any time.
The fundamental rule of this game is: all players with free feet must keep moving!

The game ends when no one, or only one person, has two free feet left.
