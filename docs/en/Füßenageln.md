# Foot Nailing

Group Size: 5+ players
Difficulty: Easy
Materials: None
Duration: 5-10 minutes

Each player tries to tap the feet of the other players with their hands. It's a free-for-all!

If a player manages to tap another player's foot, that foot is "nailed" to the ground. This means the foot cannot be moved from its spot. However, players can still try to dodge with their other, free foot. Additionally, players can tap other feet at any time.
The basic rule for this game is: all free players must keep moving!

The game ends when no one, or only one person, has two free feet left.
