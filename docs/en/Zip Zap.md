# Zip Zap

Zip Zap

A game for any group size:

Players stand in a circle.

There are four different impulses: "Zip" (to the right), "Zap" (to the left), "Peng" (to any person in the circle), and "Boing" (to bounce the impulse back).

Example flow:

The facilitator starts by clapping a "Zip" to the person on their right. That person passes it on with a "Zip." The third person points at someone else in the circle and says "Peng." The person who was "shot" raises their arms, bends their stomach forward, says "Boing," and bounces the impulse back.

Now, the third person passes the impulse to the left, saying "Zap"...

The game can be played for any length of time, for example, until the impulse returns to the facilitator.
