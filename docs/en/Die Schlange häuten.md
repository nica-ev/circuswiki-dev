# Shedding the Snake

A simple movement game for small to medium-sized groups.

Everyone stands in a line with their legs spread apart.
Everyone bends forward so that they can reach through their own legs with their right hand and grasp the left hand of the person behind them.

The last person in line lies down on the ground (hands can be released).
The others now move backward together (without letting go of hands) until the "new" last person has a place to lie down.

The game ends when everyone is lying down.

As a variation, you can also form an endless circle by having the people lying down stand up again once everyone else has walked over them, and then rejoin the line at the front.
