# Sugar Water Shootout

**Group Size**: 6 to 20 players
**Difficulty**: Slightly challenging
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 7 minutes

**Game Description**:
All players have an imaginary water gun filled with sugar water. Since sugar water is incredibly sticky in hair, nobody wants to get hit. One player starts by calling out the name of another player in the circle who is not their immediate neighbor. Simultaneously, they "shoot" at that player with their imaginary gun. The targeted player must duck as quickly as possible. If they fail to duck in time, they are hit and eliminated. However, if they duck successfully, the two players sitting next to the targeted player must duel each other. They also have to "shoot" at each other by calling out the other's name as quickly as possible. The player who loses the duel sits down. Now, it's the turn of the player who was originally targeted. They can then call out another player's name (again, not their neighbor) and "shoot" at them. As players are eliminated, everyone's neighbors change, and they have to call out different names. This game is excellent for memorizing everyone's names.
