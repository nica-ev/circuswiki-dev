# The Hurdle

**Group Size:** 4 to 20 players
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Materials:** Rope
**Playtime:** 5 to 10 minutes

**Game Description:**
A rope is stretched taut between two fixed posts, poles, trees, or similar objects. The game leader must carefully judge the height at which the rope is strung. A player should be able to jump over the rope on their own. The goal of the game is to get everyone over the rope without touching it or going under it. This means players must lift and carry each other over the rope, build human pyramids, or use "robber's ladders" (a technique where one person supports another's feet to help them climb over). Each group will find its own way to get everyone across the rope. If any player touches the rope, everyone must return to the starting side.
