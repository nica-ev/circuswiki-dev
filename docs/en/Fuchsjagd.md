# Fox Hunt

> [!info] Short Description
> A simple and dynamic chase game for small to medium-sized groups, where the goal is to snatch the "fox tails" of others while avoiding being caught yourself.

**Group Size**: Small to Medium
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Materials**: One cloth (as a "fox tail") for each participant
**Game Duration**: Approx. 10-15 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Each participant tucks a cloth (as a "fox tail") into the back of their pants. Only the "fox" (the person who is "it") starts without a tail.

The fox then tries to snatch a fox tail from another player. If successful, the player whose tail was snatched becomes the new fox.
