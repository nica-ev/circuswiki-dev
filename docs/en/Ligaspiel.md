# Game Introduction: "League Play"

Participants compete one-on-one against everyone in their group.

Even if you lose a match, it's not the end of the game.

Participants play an equal number of games and compete for points.

This means you get to participate in many games.

**[Preparation]**

Create a ranking board for groups of 5 to 9 people, listing all participants' names.

(For example, if there are 18 participants, form 3 groups of 6 people each.)

A sample ranking board will be provided.

**[Scoring]** *As a guideline

- 3 points: Win
- 2 points: Win (Riki)
- 1 point: Draw (Packan), Win from the ground
- 0 points: Lose, Lose (Riki), Lose from the ground

**Special Rules** Beginners may have **special rules**, such as receiving 1 point for going to the ground and even spinning a little, even if they lose the game.

**[Gameplay Flow]**

1.  **Within each game group**, decide on a referee and how the game will progress.

2.  **One-on-one match:** Throw only once (restart only if both players lift off the ground). When a match is completed, record the points for each player on the board.

3.  **Repeat step 2 within the group.** Participate in a round-robin format, tallying points once all matches are finished.

4.  **Finals:** The top scorers from each group will compete to determine the overall winner.

**[Caution]**

- The ranking board will have rows and columns. Record the result of a win or loss based on the row.

- Try to play in a balanced way so that the same person doesn't play consecutively.

- If you call out the next opponent and have them ready, the match can proceed smoothly.
