# Pictures

An activity for groups of approximately 4-8 people.

## Stage I

Groups take turns, having 7 seconds to create a "picture" with their bodies.

The prompt can be public for everyone or secret for the other groups. In the latter variation, we play group charades.

Example prompts:

*   Flowerpot
*   Flower
*   Umbrella
*   Car
*   Table
*   Comb
*   Mixer
*   Mug
*   Plate

## Stage II

We add 3-7 seconds to the countdown. The goal and rules are the same, but the group can use circus props.
