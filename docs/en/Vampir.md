# Vampire

Vampire

*   Everyone closes their eyes.
*   One "Head Vampire" is chosen before the game begins.
*   When a vampire encounters a non-vampire, they place their hands around the non-vampire's neck, shout, and thus infect them (the non-vampire then becomes a vampire).
*   If two vampires meet (both place their hands around the other's neck), they both transform back into non-vampires.
*   Only the Head Vampire cannot be transformed back.

The goal is to turn everyone into vampires.
