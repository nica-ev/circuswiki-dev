> [!info] Short Description
> A game played with eyes closed where vampires can infect others and revert to human form when they meet each other.

**Group Size**: 6-30 people
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: None
**Game Duration**: 5-10 minutes

## **Game Description**:

*   Everyone plays with their eyes closed.
*   One player is designated as the "Head Vampire" before the game begins.
*   When a vampire meets a non-vampire, they place their hands around the other person's neck, shout, and thus infect them (the non-vampire becomes a vampire).
*   If two vampires meet (each placing their hands around the other's neck), they both revert to non-vampires.
*   Only the Head Vampire cannot be reverted.

The goal is to turn everyone into vampires.
