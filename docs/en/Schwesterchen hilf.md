# **Sister, Help Me**

**Group Size**: 8 or more players, preferably an even number
**Difficulty**: Simple
**Materials**: None
**Playing Time**: Approx. 5 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Each player finds a partner and stands next to them. One pair becomes the "chaser-runner" pair, and the chaser begins to chase the runner. If the chaser succeeds, the roles switch. However, the runner can save themselves by standing at the side of another pair. In this case, the person on the outside of that pair becomes the new chaser, and the original chaser becomes the runner. For even more movement, multiple chaser-runner pairs can be designated, but each pair only chases within their own group.
