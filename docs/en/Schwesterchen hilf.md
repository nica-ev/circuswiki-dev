> [!info] Brief Description
> A partner tag game where the runner attaches themselves to a pair, creating new roles.

**Group Size**: 8+ players, preferably an even number
**Difficulty**: Simple
**Materials**: None
**Playtime**: Approx. 5 minutes

## **Game Description**:

Each player finds a partner and stands next to them. One pair becomes the "catcher-runner" pair, and the catcher begins chasing the runner. If the catcher tags the runner, their roles switch. However, the runner can save themselves by standing next to any other pair. When this happens, the person on the outside of that pair becomes the new catcher, and the original catcher becomes the runner. For even more activity, multiple catcher-runner pairs can be designated, but each pair must remain separate from the others.
