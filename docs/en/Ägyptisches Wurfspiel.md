> [!info] Brief Description
> This throwing game for small groups, likely originating in Egypt, challenges concentration and reaction time. The goal is to throw the ball to the correct person while the opposing group tries to avoid mistakes.

**Group Size**: 4 players (2 teams of 2 people) or 8, 12, 16, etc.
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: 1 ball
**Game Duration**: Variable (depending on the target score)

## **Game Description**:

The basic structure of the game is thought to originate from Egypt. Two groups, each consisting of two people, face each other.

One person from a group (Group 1) has a ball. One person from the opposing group (Group 2) must then clap. It's important that the people within a group do not coordinate or communicate extensively.

The person with the ball from Group 1 must now throw it to the person in Group 2 who did *not* clap.

A group scores a point when:

*   the other group fails to catch the ball
*   the other group reacts too slowly
*   the other group throws the ball incorrectly (i.e., to the person who clapped)

The group that reaches a predetermined score first wins the game.

The game can likely be played with more people, but it is generally suitable for very small groups.
