> [!info] Brief Description
> A cooperative task where the group sorts themselves on chairs according to criteria, without touching the floor.

**Group Size**: 5-25 people
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Materials**: Chairs or stools
**Playtime**: 5-15 minutes

## **Game Description**:

A cooperative game for small to medium-sized groups.

Props: Chairs or stools, etc., one for each person.

The chairs are placed in a circle with a small gap between them, allowing people to comfortably move from one chair to the next.

Each person now steps onto a chair. The task is for the group to sort themselves without anyone touching the floor.

Sorting can be done, for example, by name, height, age, etc.

To make it more challenging, verbal communication can be forbidden.
Placing the chairs in a line instead of a circle also makes the task more difficult.
